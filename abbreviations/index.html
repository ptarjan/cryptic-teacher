Cryptic crossword abbreviations

A cryptic clue's wordplay is built out of pieces, and the smallest pieces are conventions rather than deductions: no amount of staring turns sailor into AB or check into CH. You either know them or you look them up, which is why they are the fastest thing a new solver can learn.

These are all 613 that appear in the puzzles annotated on this site, listed word first — the direction you arrive from. New ones are added automatically as they turn up in a clue.

490 of them link to a clue that uses the convention, with the whole of its wordplay taken apart underneath — a convention is easier to keep once you have seen a setter use it. The rest are waiting for a clue we have annotated to need them.
