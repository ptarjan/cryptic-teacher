Across

1 across RAVIOLI

Pasta recipe? Voila! Introduction to Italian cooking (7)

Definition: Pasta · anagram + charade + first letter

  • recipe → R R is the recipe abbreviation, the one that heads a prescription
  • Voila → VOILA five of the six letters that go into the pan
  • Italian → I the introduction to ITALIAN supplies the sixth

R for recipe is a chemist's convention crosswords borrowed long ago. Note where this puzzle is set: nearly every surface from here on is a kitchen, and the food words are usually the fodder rather than the meaning.

5 across VITAMIN

Something good for you, varied in taste, taking beginners one minute (7)

Definition: Something good for you · charade + first letters

  • varied in taste → VIT the beginners of Varied In Taste
  • one → A 'a' is one, as in a minute meaning one minute
  • minute → MIN MIN is the everyday abbreviation for minute

Three initials and then two abbreviations: the answer is made entirely of shorthand, which is exactly why the surface can stay so smooth.

9 across TENDERISING

Assemble ingredients making chewing easier (11)

Definition: making chewing easier · anagram

  • ingredients → INGREDIENTS eleven letters, exactly the enumeration
  • Assemble → the anagram indicator, disguised as a recipe instruction

The whole clue reads as a line from a cookbook, which is the trap: 'Assemble' is telling you to rearrange letters, not to cook anything.

10 across PEA

Chop the end off fruit and vegetable (3)

Definition: vegetable · deletion

  • fruit → PEA the fruit is a PEAR, and the chop takes its final R away

Pear and pea sit one letter apart, which is the whole clue. 'Chop the end off' is doing kitchen duty on the surface and letter duty underneath.

11 across OVAL

Flavour comes back somewhat similar to an egg (4)

Definition: similar to an egg · hidden word + reversal

  • Flavour → LAVO f-LAVO-ur holds the letters, and an across entry coming back reads them right to left

Reversed hidden words are the hardest to spot, because the letters look wrong until you read the other way. 'Somewhat' is the tell that only part of a word is wanted.

12 across DILETTANTI

Enthusiasts of diet sandwiches left thank-you note before tea delivered (10)

Definition: Enthusiasts · container + charade + homophone

  • diet → DIET the word that gets sliced open after its second letter
  • left → L L is the standard abbreviation for left, and it is the filling
  • thank-you → TA TA is the informal thank-you
  • note → N N abbreviates note
  • tea → TI the sol-fa note TI is written differently but said exactly like 'tea'

The answer keeps its Italian plural: one dilettante, several dilettanti. 'Delivered' is the sound instruction — a speech is delivered aloud.

13 across AMONTILLADO

Sherry in the morning acceptable before a party (11)

Definition: Sherry · charade

  • in the morning → AM ante meridiem, the hours before noon
  • acceptable → ON the British idiom — something unacceptable is 'just not on'
  • before → TILL till and before both mean up to a point in time
  • a → A the letter lifted straight out of the clue
  • party → DO a do is a party, another crossword regular

Five pieces, none longer than four letters, and every one of them an everyday word — the difficulty is entirely in seeing where to cut.

18 across AMYLOPECTIN

May unfortunately see can contains rejected mushroom starch component (11)

Definition: starch component · anagram + charade + container + reversal

  • May → AMY the three letters of MAY shuffled
  • see → LO LO is the archaic 'see', as in lo and behold
  • mushroom → PEC a CEP is the mushroom, and 'rejected' turns it round
  • can → TIN a can is a tin

CEP for a mushroom and LO for 'see' are the two bits of crossword vocabulary to bank here. Amylopectin is the branched half of starch; its partner is amylose.

21 across OVERSLEEPS

Finished small pastries at last after flipping flake fails to rise (10)

Definition: fails to rise · charade + reversal + last letter

  • Finished → OVER when something is finished it is over
  • small → S S is the clothing-label abbreviation for small
  • flake → LEEP paint that flakes PEELs, and 'flipping' turns it round
  • pastries at last → S the final letter of pastrieS

'Flake' as a verb — what old paint does — is the step to find, not a person or a chocolate bar. The definition is delightfully literal.

23 across SCAR

Spoil escargot partway through (4)

Definition: Spoil · hidden word

  • escargot → SCAR e-SCAR-got: the answer sits inside the snail

One word long enough to bury a four-letter answer is all a hidden clue needs, and an unusual menu item is always a good place to start looking.

25 across ICI

Chemical company at one time dissolving biscuit in odd locations (1,1,1)

Definition: Chemical company at one time · alternate letters

  • biscuit → ICI B, S, U and T sit in the odd positions of BISCUIT and dissolve away

Alternate-letter clues are usually flagged by 'odd' or 'even'; here 'odd locations' names the letters that GO, not the ones that stay.

26 across CARBONISING

Char occasionally good, as the French say, in emerging "extreme grilling" (11)

Definition: extreme grilling · container + charade + alternate letters

  • Char occasionally → CA the alternate letters of C-h-A-r
  • emerging → RISING something emerging is rising
  • good, as the French say → BON BON is French for good, and it drops in behind the R

'Char' looks like the definition and is really the fodder — the setter has put the cooking word exactly where you expect the meaning to be.

27 across DISUSED

Deli on the fringes marinated missing duck left to rot (7)

Definition: left to rot · charade + outer letters + deletion

  • Deli → DI the fringes of DELI, its outer letters
  • marinated → SUSED marinated is SOUSED, with the duck's O gone missing
  • duck → O a duck is a zero in cricket, hence the letter O

Duck for O comes from cricket's scoreboard and is one of the most useful conventions there is. SOUSED means both pickled and drunk, which the surface enjoys.

28 across STARTLE

Surprise! After brief wait trifle, if dropped, reconstituted (7)

Definition: Surprise · charade + anagram + deletion

  • brief wait → STA a wait is a STAY, and 'brief' clips its last letter
  • trifle, if dropped → TRLE TRIFLE loses its I and its F, leaving four letters to rebuild

A dropped trifle is a fine picture of a surprise, and it is the instruction too: the letters I and F fall out of the word before the rest is put back together.

Down

1 down RATIONAL

Reasonable portion: a large (8)

Definition: Reasonable · charade

  • portion → RATION a ration is a measured portion of food
  • a → A the letter lifted straight out of the clue
  • large → L L is the clothing-size abbreviation for large

The colon does the misdirection: it makes 'Reasonable portion' read as one phrase, when the definition stops after the very first word.

2 down VINDALOO

In half-dark toilet after very hot curry (8)

Definition: hot curry · charade + first letters

  • very → V V is the standard abbreviation for very
  • In → IN the word taken as it stands
  • half-dark → DA the first half of DARK
  • toilet → LOO LOO is the everyday British toilet, and a crossword workhorse

V for very and LOO for toilet are two abbreviations worth banking. The queasy joke the surface is making about a vindaloo and a lavatory is doing all the misdirection.

3 down OVEN

After the start, adore new kitchen appliance (4)

Definition: kitchen appliance · charade + deletion

  • adore → OVE to adore is to LOVE, and everything after its start is OVE
  • new → N N is the standard abbreviation for new

'After the start' is a beheading instruction wearing ordinary clothes — in a puzzle full of recipes it reads as a note about timing.

4 down INITIALLY

Egg feeding two in Rome completely unknown as a starter (9)

Definition: as a starter · container + charade

  • two in Rome → II II is the Roman numeral two, and it splits to take a filling
  • Egg → NIT a nit is a louse's egg
  • completely → ALL ALL does duty for completely, as in 'all done'
  • unknown → Y algebra's unknown quantities are X, Y and Z

The egg here is the louse's, not the hen's — NIT is the crossword staple, and 'feeding' is a swallowing instruction rather than a meal.

5 down VOICE

Utter failing to eat nothing (5)

Definition: Utter · container

  • failing → VICE a vice is a moral failing
  • nothing → O O is the round nothing, zero

'Utter' is the verb, not the adjective — you utter and you voice an opinion. Eating as an instruction to swallow one piece inside another is worth banking.

6 down TIGHTROPE

Blending hot tripe with icing in the end? This creates a problem with balance (9)

Definition: This creates a problem with balance · anagram + last letter

  • hot tripe → HOTTRIPE eight of the nine letters, waiting to be blended
  • icing → G the end of ICING supplies the ninth

The definition works by consequence rather than description: a tightrope is not itself a problem with balance, it is the thing that sets one.

7 down MOPING

In a stew, brief period before microwave's indication that it's finished (6)

Definition: In a stew · charade

  • brief period → MO a mo is a moment — old slang crosswords keep alive
  • microwave's indication that it's finished → PING the ping a microwave sounds when its timer runs out

The long second fragment is a description rather than a synonym: there is no abbreviation to spot, just the noise the machine makes.

8 down NIACIN

Something in fish for example I can cut up at home (6)

Definition: Something in fish for example · anagram + charade

  • I can → NIAC the four letters of I CAN rearranged
  • at home → IN someone at home is in

'Cut up' reads as kitchen work and is really the shuffle instruction — the game this whole puzzle plays. Niacin is vitamin B3.

14 down TRANSACTS

Deals with type of unhealthy fat, then gets down to business (9)

Definition: Deals with · charade

  • type of unhealthy fat → TRANS trans fats are the notoriously unhealthy kind
  • gets down to business → ACTS to act is to stop deliberating and do something

Both halves are ordinary English doing double duty: 'trans' is only half a word until the fat names it, and 'acts' is buried in a business idiom.

15 down AMORPHOUS

Lacking a clear plan, prepare ham or soup (9)

Definition: Lacking a clear plan · anagram

  • ham or soup → HAMORSOUP nine letters of lunch, exactly the enumeration
  • prepare → the anagram indicator, reading as kitchen work

AMORPHOUS literally means without shape, from the Greek morphe. Watch how 'or' sits inside the fodder and looks like a choice you are being offered.

16 down STOCKIST

Supplier's broth is nutritional at the core (8)

Definition: Supplier · charade + middle letter

  • broth → STOCK stock is the broth a soup is built on
  • is → IS the word taken as it stands
  • nutritional → T the middle letter of the eleven in NUTRITIONAL

That 's is only joinery, not a possessive to unpick — spotting it is half the battle. The pleasure is STOCK doing duty as both broth and shop inventory.

17 down INTRIGUE

Secretly plan hot, dependable stuffing with essence of figs (8)

Definition: Secretly plan · container + charade

  • hot → IN hot in the fashionable sense is IN
  • dependable → TRUE dependable is TRUE, and it is about to be stuffed
  • figs → IG the essence — the middle — of fIGs

'Hot' meaning fashionable is the step to find, and the comma sitting after it is doing its best to make you read heat instead.

19 down HONIED

Sweet made sharper with addition of iodine (6)

Definition: Sweet · container

  • made sharper → HONED to hone a blade is to make it sharper
  • iodine → I I is iodine's chemical symbol

HONIED is the older spelling of honeyed and perfectly sound; the crossing letters are what tell you which of the two the setter wants.

20 down SERIES

Course is enough for one doing the work of five (6)

Definition: Course · substitution

  • is enough for → SERVES if a thing serves, it is enough for the purpose
  • one → I the Roman numeral one, which moves in
  • five → V the Roman numeral five, whose job I takes over

The clue describes a job swap and means a letter swap: Roman numerals let 'one doing the work of five' be read as I standing in for V.

22 down EARED

Burned when initially forgotten, as cereal typically is (5)

Definition: as cereal typically is · deletion

  • Burned → EARED burned is SEARED, and forgetting its initial S leaves EARED

Cereals grow in ears, which is the sense wanted — nothing here to do with hearing. 'Initially forgotten' is a beheading instruction, not a memory lapse.

24 down PITA

Bread on American menus from Pisa - feta not reliable (4)

Definition: Bread on American menus · deletion

  • Pisa - feta → PITA PISAFETA with S, A, F and E struck out leaves P-I-T-A standing in order
  • reliable → SAFE reliable is SAFE, the four letters that 'not' removes

Nothing is shuffled here: strike the letters of SAFE out of the town and the cheese and the answer survives in its original order.
