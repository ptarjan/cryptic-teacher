Across

1 across ERGOMANIACS

Americans mad about work, as this lot are (11)

Definition: as this lot are · anagram + container

  • Americans → AMERICANS nine letters of raw material, not a nationality
  • work → GO a machine with plenty of go is one that works, and the pair sits inside the shuffle

'Americans' looks like the subject of the sentence and is really the fodder. The closing words point back at the people the clue has just described, which is where the definition hides.

7 across OHM

No shame occasionally offering some resistance (3)

Definition: some resistance · alternate letters

  • No shame → OHM every second letter of the two words, counting from the second

The surface is a shameless heckler standing his ground. 'Some' is the quiet signal that the definition wants a measure rather than an act of defiance.

9 across TASER

A weapon was tested again after backfiring (5)

Definition: A weapon · reversal

  • was tested again → RESAT to resit an exam is to take it again, and the clue wants the past tense

The exam sense of 'was tested again' is the way in, while the surface keeps you on a firing range watching a prototype being trialled.

10 across NAOMI WOLF

Follow one complaint about controversial feminist author (5,4)

Definition: controversial feminist author · charade + reversal

  • Follow → FLOW what flows from an action follows from it, which is the sense being leaned on
  • one → I the Roman numeral for one
  • complaint → MOAN a moan is a grumble or complaint

The equation of following with flowing is the setter's stretch, and it is the only step here that is not straight synonym work; everything else turns on reading the assembly backwards.

11 across CRUSADING

American bill to stop college group campaigning (9)

Definition: campaigning · charade + container

  • college → C C is the standing abbreviation for college
  • group → RING a ring is a group acting together, usually with something to hide
  • American → US US for the United States, the usual shorthand for American
  • bill → AD a bill posted on a wall is an advertisement

The surface is a legislature clamping down on student protest, so 'bill' looks like the parliamentary kind. It is the sort you paste up, and 'stop' is what you do to a gap.

12 across PLUTO

What was foreshadowed by Percival Lowell initially before bowing out? (5)

Definition: What was foreshadowed · charade + first letters + anagram

  • Percival Lowell initially → PL the opening letters of the two names, which are also the astronomer's initials
  • out → UTO the three letters of OUT, bent into a different order

Those two initials were built into the name deliberately when the planet was found, in Lowell's honour. 'Bowing out' catches the demotion to dwarf planet that came later.

13 across ROAD HOG

Reckless driver runs over lad, out of his head when approaching corner (4,3)

Definition: Reckless driver · charade + deletion

  • runs → R R is the cricket scoreboard's abbreviation for runs
  • lad → AD LAD shown without the letter that heads it
  • his head → the head of LAD is its first letter, L, and that is the one removed
  • corner → HOG to corner a market is to hog it, taking far more than a fair share

Cricket supplies the two single letters, an over being the six balls bowled from one end. 'Corner' is the verb, which is where the greed in the surface comes from.

15 across TRIP

Heads off to Ramallah in Palestine for vacation? (4)

Definition: vacation · first letters

  • to Ramallah in Palestine → TRIP each of the four words surrenders the letter it begins with

'Heads off' reads as somebody setting out, which is the whole trick of the surface: it is really the instruction telling you to collect initials.

18 across ONAN

Odd parts declined by John Wayne? He pulled out at the last minute (4)

Definition: He pulled out at the last minute · alternate letters

  • John Wayne → ONAN read the two names as one run and keep the letters in even positions

The film star is there for the surface, a leading man turning down a part, and for his letters. The definition is the Genesis story told with a straight face.

20 across TIGHTEN

Announcement of giant squeeze (7)

Definition: squeeze · homophone

  • giant → TIGHTEN a titan is a giant, after the elder gods of Greek myth, and said aloud it is one letter away

'Squeeze' is the verb, not the noun: you tighten a grip rather than give one. The surface, meanwhile, is a bear hug from somebody enormous.

23 across PURGE

Pressure applied to city of old Georgia to eliminate dissidents? (5)

Definition: to eliminate dissidents · charade

  • Pressure → P P is the scientist's symbol for pressure
  • city of old → UR Ur was a great city of ancient Sumer, in what is now southern Iraq
  • Georgia → GE GE is the international code for the country of Georgia

Two Georgias are in play and the clue wants the Caucasus one, not the American state. The question mark flags a definition by example.

24 across IMPERFECT

Tense setter is cutting short cigar (9)

Definition: Tense · charade + deletion

  • setter is → IM the setter writing the clue speaks as I, so 'setter is' becomes I'm
  • cigar → PERFECT a perfecto is a cigar tapered at both ends, docked here of its last letter

'Tense' is the grammarian's noun and not an adjective about nerves, which is the whole misdirection: the surface has a nervous smoker.

26 across CAPRICORN

Island to the west of my new house (9)

Definition: house · charade

  • Island → CAPRI Capri is the island in the Bay of Naples
  • my → COR 'Cor!' is a British exclamation of surprise doing the same job as 'My!'
  • new → N N is the standard abbreviation for new

Two small words carry the clue: 'house' is the astrological term, and 'my' is an exclamation rather than a possessive.

27 across IRENE

Descartes declaring himself the personification of peace (5)

Definition: the personification of peace · charade

  • declaring himself → I the philosopher's famous declaration starts from the first person, one letter long
  • Descartes → RENE his forename was Rene, spelt with an acute accent the grid cannot hold

Cogito ergo sum, I think therefore I am, is the declaration being pointed at, and the forename supplies the rest of a Greek goddess.

28 across EVE

Scrumper evicting companion finally from flat (3)

Definition: Scrumper · deletion + last letter

  • flat → EVE the four-letter word for level, docked of the letter it ends with
  • companion finally → the last letter of companion is N, and that is the one evicted

Scrumping is stealing apples out of somebody else's orchard, which is the joke: the first apple ever taken was taken in Eden. 'Flat' is the level sense.

29 across HAEMOPHILIA

Henry recollected Ophelia and Mia having problem with the Victoria Line? (11)

Definition: problem with the Victoria Line · anagram

  • Henry → H H is the symbol for the henry, the unit of inductance
  • Ophelia and Mia → OPHELIAMIA ten more letters of fodder, run together as they stand

'The Victoria Line' is not the Underground. The disorder ran down Queen Victoria's descendants into the royal families of Russia, Spain and Prussia.

Down

1 down ENTICERS

Those who lure models in secret (8)

Definition: Those who lure · anagram

  • in secret → INSECRET eight letters of fodder, taken exactly as the clue writes them

The surface has glamorous models luring somebody quietly. The instruction is that same word as a verb, the shaping kind rather than the catwalk kind.

2 down GESTURAL

Mixture of lust and rage expressed in body movements (8)

Definition: expressed in body movements · anagram

  • lust and rage → LUSTRAGE the two nouns supply all eight letters between them

Desire and anger read as a state of mind in the surface. The answer is about how feelings show instead: hands, shoulders and face rather than words.

3 down MARIA

She has the Man in the Moon's darkish features (5)

Definition: She · double definition

  • She → a very common woman's given name, the Latin and Italian form of Mary
  • the Man in the Moon's darkish features → the dark patches that make up the face on the Moon are flat lava plains

Two senses meet in five letters. The astronomical one is a plural: each dark plain is a mare, Latin for sea, because early observers took them for water.

4 down NANKING

Old lady's man on board in Chinese port (7)

Definition: Chinese port · charade

  • Old lady → NAN nan is the familiar British word for a grandmother
  • man on board → KING the board is a chessboard, and the man on it that the whole game turns on

'Man on board' wants to be a sailor and is a chess piece. The city is spelt Nanjing today; crosswords still like the older transliteration.

5 down AMONGST

Song cycles "about" a mountain? On the contrary, it's "in" (7)

Definition: "in" · cycling + container

  • a → A the single letter, taken as it stands
  • mountain → MT MT is the map-maker's abbreviation for mount
  • Song cycles → ONGS SONG sends its opening letter round to the back

The clue quotes its own instructions. Read straight, it would wrap the mountain round the cycled letters; the middle phrase turns that arrangement inside out.

6 down STIPPLING

Son starts drinking and painting in dotty fashion (9)

Definition: painting in dotty fashion · charade + first letter

  • Son starts → S the opening letter of Son
  • drinking → TIPPLING to tipple is to drink, steadily and habitually

'Dotty' is doing two jobs at once: it keeps the surface silly, and it names the technique precisely, since the paint really does go on as separate dots.

7 down OBOLUS

Round lump sum of money in ancient Greece (6)

Definition: sum of money in ancient Greece · charade

  • Round → O a round shape drawn on the page is the letter O
  • lump → BOLUS a bolus is a soft rounded lump, of chewed food or of medicine

Worth banking: crosswords are fond of ancient money, and this coin was one sixth of a drachma, the fare traditionally left for the ferryman.

8 down MY FOOT

Well I never pay for rubbish! (2,4)

Definition: rubbish! · charade

  • Well I never → MY 'My!' on its own is the same little cry of astonishment
  • pay for → FOOT to foot the bill is to pay it

Read as one sentence it is a shopper refusing to be overcharged. Read as a clue it is three exclamations in a row, two of them building the third.

14 down HINDEMITH

German scorer in game has hard time making a comeback (9)

Definition: German scorer · charade + reversal

  • game → HIND a hind is a female red deer, and deer are game
  • hard → H H is the grading stamped on a hard pencil
  • time → TIME taken as it stands, ready to be turned round with the letter before it

'German scorer' wants to be a footballer and 'in game' plays along with that. The scorer is a composer, and the game is the four-legged kind.

16 down ETHEREAL

That place in east Alabama is heavenly (8)

Definition: heavenly · charade + container

  • east → E E is the compass abbreviation for east
  • That place → THERE the everyday word for a place already pointed at
  • Alabama → AL AL is the US postal abbreviation for Alabama

The surface plants you in the American South, where the state is worth only two letters. The definition is the airy, unearthly sense, not praise for the scenery.

17 down ANATHEMA

The curse of Bibiana the Martyr (8)

Definition: The curse · hidden word

  • Bibiana the Martyr → ANATHEMA the letters run consecutively across the join between the saint's name and her title

Saint Bibiana was an early Roman martyr, and she is here only because her name and title happen to run together the right way.

19 down NOISOME

Moonie's bum is foul-smelling (7)

Definition: foul-smelling · anagram

  • Moonie's → MOONIES seven letters of fodder, the apostrophe ignored

A Moonie is a follower of the Unification Church, founded by Sun Myung Moon. The surface is rude about the sect; the setter only wants the letters.

20 down TAP INTO

I'm grateful for that piebald hack (3,4)

Definition: hack · charade

  • I'm grateful for that → TA ta is the everyday British word for thanks
  • piebald → PINTO a pinto is a horse marked in patches of white and one other colour

The surface is a scruffy horse, and a hack is a riding horse too, which is the trap. The definition is the computing sense.

21 down SPECIE

Change in pieces? (6)

Definition: Change · anagram

  • pieces → PIECES the six letters that get rearranged, doing double duty as the instruction

The question mark covers the wink. Coined money really is change in pieces, so the clue describes its own answer while telling you what to do to the letters.

22 down TRIPLE

Backside of prominent Liberal observed in mature threesome (6)

Definition: threesome · charade + container + last letter

  • Backside of prominent → T the last letter of prominent
  • mature → RIPE fruit that has matured is ripe
  • Liberal → L L is the standard abbreviation for Liberal in politics

Wiglaf's surface reads as gossip about a politician being watched. Underneath, 'mature' is the fruit sense and the Liberal is reduced to a party initial.

25 down REITH

Old director general punches where it hurts (5)

Definition: Old director general · hidden word

  • where it hurts → REITH the letters run on unbroken through the middle of the phrase, across two word breaks

John Reith was the BBC's first Director-General, and his name still labels high-minded broadcasting: the annual Reith Lectures are his memorial. The surface is a boxing report.
