1 across LOHENGRIN

Rheingold mostly replaced by new Wagner opera (9)

Definition: Wagner opera · anagram + deletion

  • Rheingold → RHEINGOL the title of the first opera in Wagner's Ring cycle, handing over all but its last letter
  • new → N N is the standard abbreviation for new

Both titles are Wagner's, so the italicised one reads as a fact to recognise rather than raw material — and it is a letter short of what is needed, which is where 'new' comes in.

6 across CLIMB

Definition: Aim of mountaineering · charade

  • clubs → C C is the card table's letter for clubs
  • member → LIMB a limb is a member of the body, the older sense of the word

The dots at the end run the sentence into the next clue and carry no letters with them. A card suit and a leg are the two senses hiding in ordinary words.

9 across GAMUT

... draw recalled around morning range (5)

Definition: range · reversal + container

  • draw → GUT to draw a rope is to tug it, and that word is set down the other way about
  • morning → AM AM marks the hours before noon on a clock

The dots carry the sentence on from the clue before and bring no letters with them. 'Draw' is a tug of war here, not a picture, which is the pivot of the whole clue.

10 across RESHUFFLE

Change of membership upset Liberal in new arrangement, mostly (9)

Definition: Change of membership · container + charade + deletion

  • new arrangement → RESE to arrange things anew is to reset them, and the last letter is left off
  • upset → HUFF somebody in a huff is upset and sulking about it
  • Liberal → L L is the party's letter on a ballot paper

'Upset' sits right beside a political party and looks like an instruction to jumble it. It is a word to translate instead, and the party gives up a single letter.

11 across THROTTLE

Definition: Suppress · charade + container + deletion

  • trio → THRE a trio is three, with the last letter of the number cut off
  • outrageous → OTT over the top, OTT for short, is what outrageous behaviour gets called
  • Latin → L L is the dictionary's tag for a Latin word

The surface is three people writing something outrageous in Latin. 'Penned' is the farmyard sense, and 'trio' is a number waiting to be spelled out.

12 across SIERRA

14 across MICRO-MINIS

  • one → I one is the Roman numeral I, a single stroke

Turning crimson is blushing on the surface, and the clue means it literally of the letters. The number in the last word is the piece they close around.

17 across TOME

Definition: a big book · charade

  • my opinion → ME the one whose opinion it is, shrunk to the speaker alone

'In my opinion' looks like the setter hedging about how big the book is. It is not a comment on the definition at all — it is the whole of the wordplay.

19 across HEARTBREAK

22 across DODGER

  • society members → ODGE a lodge is a branch of a society such as the freemasons, and the word covers its members too; the front letter goes

The surface is a doctor keeping a club's names quiet. A lodge is a local branch of a society and also the people in it, which is what makes 'society members' fair.

23 across VISCOUNT

Reduced price: initial 99% reduction for this nobleman (8)

Definition: nobleman · substitution + first letter

  • Reduced price → DISCOUNT a reduction in price is a discount, and this is the word being worked on
  • initial → D the letter that word opens with, which doubles as the Roman numeral for five hundred
  • 99% reduction → V take ninety-nine per cent off five hundred and five is left, which the Romans wrote V

'Reduced price' looks like the definition of a bargain and is really the word being altered, and the arithmetic is Roman, which is what lets a percentage act on a letter.

25 across ALLEVIATE

Definition: Cushion · charade + deletion

  • a → A the article taken as the single letter it is written with
  • large → L L is the size on a clothing label
  • rise → LEVIATE to levitate is to rise off the ground, and one of its two Ts is dropped
  • time → T t is the scientist's symbol for time, and it is the letter taken out

'A large rise' sounds like a pay claim and 'ignoring first time' like a lenient magistrate. Both are instructions about letters, and neither is about money or mercy.

28 across THREATENS
