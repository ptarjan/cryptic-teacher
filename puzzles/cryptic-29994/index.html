1 across APPEASE

Take off clothing quietly – like sex naked and calm (7)

Definition: calm · container + charade + middle letter

To appease is to calm somebody down by giving them what they want, so 'calm' is the verb it stands in for.

  • Take off → APE to take somebody off is to ape them, copying their voice and manner
  • quietly → P p is the musician's mark for piano, meaning play it softly
  • like → AS 'as' does the job of 'like' in a comparison: quick as a flash
  • sex naked → E take the letters off either end of the word and its middle letter is what is left
  • clothing — To clothe a thing is to put a covering right round it, so the word in front closes over the letter named next.
  • naked — Something naked has had its outer covering stripped off, so the word loses the letters at both ends and only its middle survives.

Clothes coming off quietly, and sex spoken of as bare and unhurried.

'Take off clothing' reads as one act of undressing, and the split really falls after 'Take off', where the sense wanted is imitate.

5 across BLIGHTY

Definition: England · container
