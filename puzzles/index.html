Cryptic crosswords, explained

Every puzzle we hold, newest first. A full hints puzzle has every clue hand-annotated: the clue family, where the definition hides, and the wordplay taken apart step by step. An answers only puzzle has the solutions but is still waiting for its annotations.

Difficulty is judged against the other puzzles here, not in the abstract — it combines how many letters the grid leaves unchecked, how rare the answers are, and which wordplay devices the setter leaned on. Start with how cryptic clues work if any of that is new.
