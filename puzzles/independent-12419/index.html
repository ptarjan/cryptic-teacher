Independent Cryptic Crossword No 12,419

Across

1 across HOUSE-TRAIN

Resort in the USA, or make Persian go elsewhere? (5-5)

Definition: make Persian go elsewhere? · anagram

  • in the USA, or → HOUSETRAIN ten letters, and not one of them stays where the clue put it

Half the clues in this grid open with 'Resort in' somewhere abroad; here the word is not naming a town at all but telling you to sort the letters again. A Persian is a cat, and 'go' is the polite verb.

7 across GOER

Traveller in Indian state picked up? (4)

Definition: Traveller · homophone

  • Indian state → GOER Goa is the small state on India's west coast, and a British speaker says it GO-uh, which is also how an unstressed -er ending comes out

Non-rhotic English is the whole trick: the r at the end is written but not sounded, so a place in India and a word for a wanderer arrive at the same noise.

9 across ALICANTE

City in Spain with the greatest specialised language: English (8)

Definition: City in Spain · charade

  • the greatest → ALI Muhammad Ali called himself The Greatest and the title stuck to him
  • specialised language → CANT cant is the private jargon of a trade or a sect, thieves' cant being the best known
  • English → E E is the usual one-letter stand-in for English

The colon does no work at all. It only lets 'specialised language: English' read as one idea, when the two halves are separate pieces of the build.

10 across CREATE

Island featuring a forge (6)

Definition: forge · container

  • Island → CRETE Crete is the big Greek island, and it opens up in the middle
  • a → A the article is taken as the single letter it is written with

A holiday island and a blacksmith make a tidy picture, and the picture is the trap: the verb at the end is the definition, not part of the scene.

11 across TIA MARIA

Italian song I adore you, having renounced love for drink (3,5)

Definition: drink · charade + deletion

  • I adore you → TIAM Italian for I love you is TI AMO, and the O has just been given up
  • Italian song → ARIA an aria is the solo song in an Italian opera

One word covers both halves of the wordplay: 'Italian' licenses the phrase TI AMO and the musical term as well. The bottle itself is Jamaican.

12 across BALTI

Indian style island welcomes first of tourists (5)

Definition: Indian style · container + first letter

  • island → BALI Bali is the Indonesian island, and it splits after its third letter
  • tourists → T the front letter of TOURISTS

The geography is a decoy. The island that opens up is Indonesian, several thousand miles from the cuisine the definition is naming.

14 across WINDSOR

Weaselly words in house (7)

Definition: house · anagram

  • words in → WINDSOR seven letters, rearranged

'Weaselly words' is a real phrase for evasive language, which is exactly what makes it invisible as an instruction — and 'in' here is fodder, not a container.

16 across CANINES

Resort in France visited by one part of the set? (7)

Definition: part of the set? · container

  • Resort in France → CANNES Cannes is the film-festival resort on the Riviera, and it opens after CAN
  • one → I one is I, the Roman numeral

Every other 'Resort in' clue in this grid is a signal to shuffle letters, so the trap here is that this one really is a town. And the set is a mouthful of teeth, not the film set Cannes has just conjured.

17 across DIAMOND

Maybe brilliant daughter without domestic staff, running about (7)

Definition: Maybe brilliant · charade + anagram

  • daughter → D D is the family-tree abbreviation for daughter
  • without domestic staff → IAMOND a domestic is a maid, so this says NO MAID — six letters that come out rearranged

'Brilliant' is an adjective on the surface and a noun in the definition, and 'without' is not a deletion here but the plain everyday word no.

18 across STREAMS

Flowers, many on street (7)

Definition: Flowers · charade

  • street → ST ST is how street is abbreviated on an envelope
  • many → REAMS reams of it means loads of it; a ream is five hundred sheets of paper

The comma after the first word is doing honest work: it fences off the definition, and the definition has nothing to do with petals.

20 across MAINE

State when I should cut hair (5)

Definition: State · container

  • hair → MANE a mane is the hair on a horse's or a lion's neck, and it opens after two letters
  • I → I the pronoun is taken as the single letter it is written with

The surface is a barber's appointment and the answer is on a map. 'Cut' is the pivot: a haircut in one reading, an incision into a word in the other.

24 across PISCES

Fishes Charlie gutted eaten by Jimmy? (6)

Definition: Fishes · container + outer letters

  • Jimmy? → PISS a Jimmy Riddle is rhyming slang for a piddle, shortened to Jimmy, and it opens up before its last letter
  • Charlie → CE CHARLIE with its insides removed keeps only its first and last letters

'Gutted' meaning emptied of insides is one of the standard signals to keep only a word's outer letters, and a clue about fish makes it almost invisible.

25 across DAINTILY

Resort in Italy paired with Germany in elegant manner (8)

Definition: in elegant manner · anagram

  • Germany → D D is Germany's international vehicle code, from Deutschland
  • in Italy → INITALY seven letters straight off the clue, joining the D in the shuffle

Three countries in one sentence and only two of them are pulling their weight: Italy for its letters, Germany for its single-letter code, and the resort is not a place at all.

27 across ONAN

Seed spiller with a very tiny part to withdraw? (4)

Definition: Seed spiller · reversal

  • a very tiny part → NANO nano- is the prefix for a thousand-millionth, the smallest fraction most people ever name

Nano- reaches English from the Greek for dwarf by way of the SI prefixes, which is a lot of scientific precision to spend on a clue whose definition comes out of Genesis.

28 across FINANCIERS

Resort in France is for Zurich gnomes? (10)

Definition: Zurich gnomes? · anagram

  • in France is → FINANCIERS ten letters and a completely new arrangement

'Is' looks like a link word holding the sentence upright and is really two more letters of the jumble — the commonest way a setter hides fodder in plain sight.

2 down OIL

Slick cat almost rampant? (3)

Definition: Slick · deletion + reversal

  • cat almost → LIO the cat is a LION and 'almost' stops one short of its N

Heraldry is where the surface and the wordplay part company: a rampant lion is a picture, and a rampant three letters is an instruction to read them the other way.

3 down SACRA

Bones in line while erect (5)

Definition: Bones · charade + reversal

  • line → ARC an arc is a line, the curved kind a compass draws
  • while → AS as in 'he whistled while he worked' — while and as both mean during the time that

Anatomy keeps its Latin plurals, which is how a definition with no S on it can still be asking for more than one bone.

4 down TANTAMOUNT

As good as knight, ultimately, soldier on a horse (10)

Definition: As good as · charade + last letter

  • knight → T the very end of KNIGHT, which is what 'ultimately' asks for
  • soldier → ANT soldier ants are the big-jawed caste of a colony, and a bare 'soldier' is the crossword's usual way of asking for one
  • a → A the article is taken at face value
  • horse → MOUNT a rider's mount is the horse being ridden

Four military-looking words in a row and the definition is the quiet phrase at the front, which is where a clue this cavalry-minded least wants you to look.

5 down ACETIC

Sharp pilot with habitual mannerism (6)

Definition: Sharp · charade

  • pilot → ACE an ace is a pilot with a tally of kills, a usage that dates from the First World War
  • habitual mannerism → TIC a tic is the twitch or catchphrase someone repeats without meaning to

The definition is a taste, not an edge — which is the whole misdirection, since 'Sharp' opening a clue reads as a knife or a mind.

6 down NICE

Resort in France worth a visit? (4)

Definition: Resort in France · double definition

  • Resort in France → NICE the Cote d'Azur town at the end of the Promenade des Anglais
  • worth a visit? → NICE somewhere worth going to is a pleasant place, and the everyday adjective for that is the same word

Punk's running gag in this grid is 'Resort in' as a signal to shuffle letters; this is the one time it means exactly what it says, and the joke is that nothing happens to it at all.

7 down GRENADINE

Killer sandwiches in syrup (9)

Definition: syrup · container

  • Killer → GRENADE a grenade is thrown to kill, and it splits open one letter from the end
  • in → IN the little word goes in whole, exactly as it is written

Grenade and grenadine share a root: both come from the pomegranate, the weapon for its shape and its scatter of seed-like fragments.

8 down ECTOTHERMIC

Adapted like frogs, say, in cold temperature: different in shivering mice (11)

Definition: Adapted like frogs, say · anagram + container

  • cold → C C is the letter on the cold tap, and the standard abbreviation for it
  • temperature → T T for temperature, as on a weather chart or in a physics formula
  • mice → MICE the rodents' four letters join the two abbreviations to make the six that get shuffled
  • different → OTHER a different one is another one — the other one

'Say' is the flag that frogs are only an example: plenty of other creatures are built the same way, and the answer is the class word covering all of them.

11 down TRINIDADIAN

Resort in India? Drat! West Indian! (11)

Definition: West Indian! · anagram

  • in India? Drat → TRINIDADIAN eleven letters, and every one of them moves

'Drat!' is a real exclamation doing a fake job: it reads as the setter's irritation at being sent to the wrong country, and it is really four more letters to shuffle.

13 down CARTHUSIAN

So boy south of Capri perhaps is brother (10)

Definition: brother · charade

  • Capri perhaps → CAR the Capri was a Ford, and 'perhaps' marks it as one example of a car
  • So → THUS thus and so are the same word for in this manner
  • boy → IAN Ian is the boy the grid wants; crosswords keep a small stable of usable names

Capri is an island and a Ford at once, which is why 'perhaps' is there — it warns you the word is an example of something rather than the thing itself.

15 down DOMINICAN REPUBLIC

Resort in Cuba priced on shortage of milk in country (9,8)

Definition: country · anagram + deletion

  • milk → MIL milk docked of its final letter leaves MIL, and those three join the letters to be sorted
  • in Cuba priced on → INCUBAPRICEDON fourteen letters taken straight off the clue, which with MIL makes seventeen

Cuba is the wrong island entirely and is here only for its letters, as is the milk. Seventeen letters is a long shuffle, so trust the enumeration rather than the surface.

19 down TENDON

Nurse touching tissue (6)

Definition: tissue · charade

  • Nurse → TEND to nurse a wound is to tend it
  • touching → ON two things touching are one on the other, so touching gives ON

Three medical-sounding words in a row and the surface never leaves the ward, so nothing in it warns you that a charade is being assembled.

22 down LATHI

Observed in Marbella, this club (5)

Definition: club · hidden word

  • Marbella, this → LATHI the last two letters of the resort run straight on into the first three of THIS

Marbella is a Spanish resort in a puzzle full of resorts, which is precisely why you read past it — and past 'this', a word that normally points at something rather than spelling it.

23 down AS IF

Conclusion yet to be reached in a riddle - yeh, right! (2,2)

Definition: yeh, right! · charade + deletion

  • a → A the article, taken as it stands
  • riddle → SIF to riddle grain is to SIFT it through a coarse sieve, and that word's ending is the conclusion never reached

'Riddle' looks like a puzzle and is really a farmyard verb, which is the one leap the clue asks for. The definition is the sarcastic exclamation at the end.

26 down LAR

Deity briefly slowing up (3)

Definition: Deity · deletion + reversal

  • slowing → RAL rallentando is the score marking for slowing down, written rall., and briefly that is RAL

Both halves are borrowed vocabulary — a Latin household god and an Italian musical instruction — which is a great deal of Europe for three letters.
