Across

9 across A FORTIORI

Defensive position adopted by excellent core of motorists with stronger reason (1,8)

Definition: with stronger reason · container + charade + middle letters

  • excellent → AI A1 means first-rate, and the 1 is written as the letter beside it on the page
  • Defensive position → FORT a fort is a defensive position
  • core of motorists → ORI the three letters at the dead centre of that nine-letter word

The surface is all traffic and defensive driving, and the answer is a Latin tag from formal argument. 'Excellent' as A1 is a standing crossword trick.

10 across ASPIC

A reduced flavouring in jelly (5)

Definition: jelly · charade + deletion

  • A → A the clue's own A leads the assembly
  • reduced flavouring → SPIC spice is the flavouring, docked of its last letter

'Reduced' is a cooking word in the surface, where a sauce is boiled down, and a shortening instruction in the parse.

11 across BOMBARD

Assail directors about quantity of computer data (7)

Definition: Assail · container

  • directors → BOARD the board is a company's directors, sitting as one body
  • quantity of computer data → MB MB is a megabyte

A boardroom and a hard drive make the surface, and the answer is artillery. Two very ordinary words hide a bombardment between them.

12 across ROSSINI

Special bit of naughtiness concealed by French king’s composer (7)

Definition: composer · container + charade

  • French king → ROI roi is French for king
  • Special → S S is the standard short form for special
  • bit of naughtiness → SIN a sin is naughtiness at its plainest

'Bit of' looks like an instruction to take a single letter; here it only means a small helping of naughtiness. The king is French and the composer Italian.

13 across IVORY

Shade of gold in plant that’s generally green or off-white (5)

Definition: Shade · container

  • plant that’s generally green or off-white → IVY ivy is the evergreen climber, and the variegated kind is cream where it is not green
  • gold → OR or is gold in heraldry, and the crossword's usual way of saying it

The definition is the very first word, which is where a solver rarely looks for one this bare. OR for gold is worth learning once and owning forever.

14 across POLICE DOG

Contributor to law enforcement chilled during game? Good (6,3)

Definition: Contributor to law enforcement · container + charade

  • game → POLO polo is the game, one example of many, which is what the question mark admits
  • chilled → ICED a chilled drink is an iced one
  • Good → G G is the standard short form for good

The surface is a text message — chilled during the game, good — and every word of it is machinery. The definition is the long phrase at the front.

15 across GONE WITH THE WIND

Merry widow and gent, eh? Hint for a film (4,4,3,4)

Definition: a film · anagram

  • widow and gent, eh? Hint → WIDOWGENTEHHINT fifteen letters of fodder out of four words, with 'and' only joining them up

'Hint for a film' reads as the setter offering help, and 'Hint' is four of the fifteen letters. The Merry Widow is an operetta, which is the picture doing the hiding.

19 across KANDINSKY

A new racket followed by broadcaster, getting behind king’s painter (9)

Definition: painter · charade

  • king → K K is the standard short form for king, as in chess notation
  • A → A the clue's own A, taken at face value
  • new → N N is the standard short form for new
  • racket → DIN a din is a racket, the noisy sense of the word
  • broadcaster → SKY Sky is the British satellite broadcaster

'Racket' is placed to look like tennis and means noise, which is the pivot the whole clue turns on. Everything named goes behind the king rather than in front.

21 across CHASM

Charles, heading for Moon and space (5)

Definition: space · charade + first letter

  • Charles → CHAS Chas is the familiar shortening of that name
  • heading for Moon → M the letter the Moon starts with

The surface sends a man to the Moon and the answer is the gap he would be crossing. Crosswords clip first names freely, so Chas is worth expecting.

22 across TABLEAU

Cross about having skill to produce picture? (7)

Definition: picture · container

  • Cross → TAU the tau cross is the T-shaped one, named for the Greek letter
  • having skill → ABLE someone having skill is able

'Cross' opens the clue looking like an angry setter about to shuffle some letters. It is a noun, and one of the crossword's regular crosses.

23 across YAWNING

Rear of shabby window-shade showing a big gap (7)

Definition: showing a big gap · charade + last letter

  • Rear of shabby → Y the last letter of that six-letter adjective
  • window-shade → AWNING an awning is the canvas shade hung over a window

The surface hangs a blind at a shabby window and nothing in it suggests sleep or a gap. An instruction sitting at the very start of a clue is easy to read as scenery.

24 across LET GO

Sack that ultimately contains bricks? On the contrary (3,2)

Definition: Sack · container + last letter

  • bricks → LEGO the plastic bricks children build with
  • that ultimately → T the last letter of the word 'that'

A brand name doing duty as ordinary bricks, and a setter admitting in three words that he has just described the containment the wrong way round.

25 across GUTENBERG

Pressman, instinctive, with book probing Green manoeuvring (9)

Definition: Pressman · charade + anagram + container

  • instinctive → GUT an instinctive reaction is a gut one
  • Green → ENERG the same five letters in a new order, opening up to take another
  • book → B B is the standard short form for book

'Pressman' reads as a reporter and the answer is the man who built the press itself, which is why the definition sits there unremarked.

1 down WARBLING

Conspicuous military decorations possibly getting you singing (8)

Definition: singing · charade

  • military → WAR the military business is war, and the word swaps straight in
  • decorations possibly → BLING bling is showy ornament worn to be noticed, and medals are one kind of decoration
  • Conspicuous → 'Conspicuous' is what turns plain decorations into the flashy sort; it hands over no letters of its own

Medals as war bling is the joke the whole surface is built on, and the definition is the single word left at the end.

2 down BOOM TOWN

A kick, after millions invested, to have a successful place (4,4)

Definition: successful place · container + charade

  • A kick → BOOT a boot is a kick
  • millions → M M is the standard short form for millions
  • to have → OWN to have something is to own it

The surface is football finance — a kick, millions invested. Read again as instructions, 'invested' is the word doing the work.

3 down STEADY

Dates ruined by variable boyfriend? (6)

Definition: boyfriend · anagram

  • Dates → DATES five of the six letters, waiting to be rearranged
  • variable → Y algebra's variables are x, y and z, and Y is the one wanted here

'Dates' looks like the romance the clue is about and is the fodder instead; the romance sits in the definition at the far end.

4 down VOID

Opening verse put at the front by Roman poet (4)

Definition: Opening · charade + deletion

  • verse → V V is the standard short form for verse
  • Roman poet → OID Ovid is the Roman poet, and this is what remains once his V has gone to the front

The poet lends his own V to the head of his name. 'Opening' reads as an adjective describing the verse and is the noun definition.

5 down HIEROLATRY

Worship of sacred figures, i.e. holy art, possibly about right (10)

Definition: Worship of sacred figures · anagram

  • i.e. holy art → IEHOLYART nine letters of fodder, with i.e. quietly giving up two of them
  • right → R R is the standard short form for right

The Greek roots are the way in: hiero- for holy things, -latry for worship, as in idolatry. The clue hands you 'holy art' as the hint.

6 down MASSACRE

Small bag fed to horse results in heavy defeat (8)

Definition: heavy defeat · container + charade

  • horse → MARE a mare is a horse, the female one
  • Small → S S is the standard short form for small, as on a clothing label
  • bag → SAC a sac is a bag, the anatomical sort

The surface is a stable at feeding time and the answer is a scoreline; the two pictures share not one word of vocabulary.

7 down UPWIND

Facing blow at university, succeed, getting degree (6)

Definition: Facing blow · charade

  • at university → UP a student in residence at Oxford or Cambridge is said to be up
  • succeed → WIN to succeed in a contest is to win it
  • getting degree → D a degree is D in the crossword's shorthand

'Blow' looks like a synonym waiting to be converted and is half the definition instead: to face the blow is to face the wind.

8 down ACHING

A system of divination disposing of one longing (6)

Definition: longing · charade + deletion

  • A → A the clue's own A opens the assembly
  • system of divination → CHING the I Ching is the Chinese book consulted for divination, arriving here short of its first letter
  • one → I one is the letter I, and it is the piece thrown away

A genuine system of divination is named and then quietly docked. 'One' as I is worth keeping — Roman numeral and pronoun at once.

14 down PITTSBURGH

City with Grub Street set up in central part (10)

Definition: City · container + reversal

  • central part → PITH the pith is the central part of a stem or a piece of fruit
  • Grub Street → TSBURG Grub Street was the London street of hack writers, ST is street as always, and the six letters go in bottom to top

The trap is reading 'central part' as an instruction to take a word's middle letters. It is a synonym, and it is the piece everything else goes inside.

16 down WHITE-HOT

Melted the Ring in a little bit, having a high temperature (5-3)

Definition: having a high temperature · charade + anagram

  • a little bit → WHIT a whit is the smallest amount there is, as in 'not a whit'
  • the Ring → EHOT the word 'the' plus the round O a ring draws, four letters run together and melted

A ring written as O is one of the conventions worth owning early. The enumeration (5-3) is the tell that this is a hyphenated pair, not one long word.

17 down IMAGINES

Believes Italy has silver in pits (8)

Definition: Believes · charade + container

  • Italy → I I is Italy, the letter on the car plates
  • pits → MINES mines are pits, the coal sort
  • silver → AG AG is the chemist's symbol for silver, from the Latin argentum

Mining country makes the surface, with Italian silver ore for colour. The definition is the first word, in the sense of supposing rather than of faith.

18 down DEMAGOGY

Populist speechifying you’d once picked up around giant figure (8)

Definition: Populist speechifying · reversal + container

  • you’d once → YED ye is the old word for you, so ye'd is how you would once have said it
  • giant figure → MAGOG Gog and Magog are the legendary giants whose statues stand in London's Guildhall

'Once' is the standard signal for an archaic form, not for a story that happened long ago — that is the reading the surface wants you to take.

19 down KETTLE

1,000 determine to avoid initial riot control area (6)

Definition: riot control area · charade + deletion

  • 1,000 → K K is a thousand, as in a 10K run or a salary
  • determine → ETTLE to settle a question is to determine it, and that verb arrives here shorn of its opening letter
  • initial → S the letter avoided: the S that the verb for determine begins with

The surface is already a police briefing, which is exactly what the definition is about — so nothing in it flags 'determine' as a synonym to be trimmed.

20 down NABOTH

Vineyard owner’s negative comment for some, about half-bottle (6)

Definition: Vineyard owner · container + deletion

  • negative comment for some → NAH nah is the casual spelling of no that some speakers favour
  • half-bottle → BOT the first three letters of the six that spell the bottle

A half-bottle is a real thing on a wine list, which is what makes the instruction invisible in a clue about a vineyard.

21 down CAWING

Crow initially flying, doing this? (6)

Definition: Crow initially flying, doing this? · &lit + charade + first letter

  • Crow initially → C the first letter of the bird's name
  • flying → AWING a bird awing is on the wing, up in the air

The question mark is the clue pointing back at itself: read straight, the whole sentence describes the noise, and read as instructions it spells it.

23 down YETI

Thing without tail, unknown climbing mountain creature (4)

Definition: mountain creature · charade + deletion + reversal

  • Thing without tail → ITE an item is a thing, and here it loses its last letter
  • unknown → Y an unknown quantity in algebra is x, y or z

The surface is a tale about some tailless animal, which is the wrong animal entirely. Y for an unknown comes from algebra and turns up constantly.
