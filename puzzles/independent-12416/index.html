Across

1 across PHONEY

Call latest from Zoffany a fake (6)

Definition: a fake · charade + last letter

  • Call → PHONE to call someone is to phone them
  • Zoffany → Y the closing letter of the painter's name

Johan Zoffany painted 18th-century society portraits, so the surface is about spotting a forged picture — but only one letter of him is wanted.

4 across HARASS

Severe cut allowing incursion by a second badger (6)

Definition: badger · container + deletion

  • Severe → HARS HARSH is severe, and the cut takes its final letter
  • a → A the article taken as its own letter
  • second → S S is the abbreviation for second, as on a stopwatch

The surface is a wildlife-crime story, and the animal at the end of it is the definition: 'badger' here is the verb.

8 across LAPTOP

Computer suitable for use in large operation (6)

Definition: Computer · container

  • large → L L is large on a clothing label
  • suitable → APT apt means suitable, well fitted to the purpose
  • operation → OP OP is the short form of operation, as in a military op
  • for use → surface only — it makes the machine sound office-ready and hands over no letters

The surface promises major surgery. Nothing here is medical: both of the outer words are ordinary crossword abbreviations.

9 across CUTWORMS

Caterpillars stayed away from historic German city (8)

Definition: Caterpillars · charade

  • stayed away from → CUT to cut a lecture, or a person, is to stay away from them deliberately
  • historic German city → WORMS Worms sits on the Rhine, and its 1521 Diet summoned Martin Luther

The city is said 'Vorms' in German, which is how a very ordinary English word manages to hide there in plain sight.

10 across CARILLON

Visit taking in first couple of ringing bells (8)

Definition: bells · container + first letters

  • Visit → CALL ON to call on a friend is to visit them
  • ringing → RI the opening pair of letters of the word, and nothing more of it

'Ringing bells' reads as one phrase, and that is the trap: only 'ringing' is fodder, and 'bells' is the definition.

11 across REIGNS

Stays in power, to abdicate after son moves (6)

Definition: Stays in power · deletion + charade

  • to abdicate → RESIGN a monarch who abdicates resigns the crown
  • son → S S is the abbreviation for son, and this is the letter that travels

Nothing is anagrammed here — one letter simply changes address, which is what lets the surface talk about a son moving.

12 across SETH

Third child fixed to get husband (4)

Definition: Third child · charade

  • fixed → SET a fixed jaw is a set one
  • husband → H H is the crossword abbreviation for husband

Genesis gives Adam and Eve a third son once Cain has killed Abel, and that is the only place a solver is meant to look.

14 across RE-EXAMINE

King with a bomb brought about end of battle? Observe again (2-7)

Definition: Observe again · container + charade + last letter

  • King → REX REX is Latin for king, the word printed on British coins
  • end of battle → E the final letter of the word battle
  • a → A the article joins on as itself
  • bomb → MINE a mine is a bomb laid in the ground or at sea

'Brought about' looks like an instruction to turn something round; here it means only 'placed around', and that difference is the whole clue.

15 across FOUR-FLUSH

Pair of couples look embarrassed? It’s the basis of a bluff (4-5)

Definition: the basis of a bluff · charade

  • Pair of couples → FOUR two couples come to four people
  • look embarrassed → FLUSH to flush is to go red in the face

'Four-flusher' passed from the poker table into American slang for a fraud, on exactly this logic.

17 across TIER

Row of seats: new design gutted after reflection (4)

Definition: Row of seats · deletion + reversal

  • new design → REFIT a refit is a new design for something that already exists — a ship, a shop
  • gutted → REIT the middle letter F is taken out of the five above

The colon hides the join: everything before it is the definition and everything after it is instruction.

18 across SURETE

Certain to involve Parisian and Calais police once (6)

Definition: Calais police once · container

  • Certain → SURE sure means certain, and it opens up to take the insert
  • Parisian and → ET 'et' is French for and, which is what a Parisian says

'Calais' is doing no more than saying 'French' — the force was national, not a local station on the coast.

19 across INTREPID

Courageous Republican probing international documents (8)

Definition: Courageous · container

  • international → INT INT is the usual short form of international
  • Republican → REP a Republican in American politics is a Rep
  • documents → ID your ID is the documents that prove who you are

The surface reads as a political scandal, a Republican digging through foreign papers, and the answer has nothing to do with politics.

22 across HIGH FIVE

Middle of night - female entering busy area - enthusiastic welcome (4,4)

Definition: enthusiastic welcome · container + middle letters

  • busy area → HIVE a hive is the busiest place there is — a hive of activity
  • night → IGH strip the outer letters off and this is what is left in the centre
  • female → F F is the standard abbreviation for female

'Middle of night' invites you to take one letter; three come out instead, which is what makes the insertion long enough to work.

23 across LAMBDA

Innocent daughter linked to a character in Homer (6)

Definition: character in Homer · charade

  • Innocent → LAMB a lamb is the proverbial innocent — like a lamb to the slaughter
  • daughter → D D is the crossword abbreviation for daughter
  • a → A the article joins on at the end as itself

The clue sends you hunting for Achilles or Penelope; Homer wrote in Greek, and the characters wanted are the ones he wrote with.

24 across MOANED

Complained article suppressed by second editor (6)

Definition: Complained · container

  • second → MO half a mo is half a moment in British speech
  • article → AN AN is one of the three articles in English grammar
  • editor → ED ED is the newsroom short form for editor

'Suppressed' looks like an instruction to delete, and that is the trap: nothing goes, the article is simply buried alive.

25 across LES SIX

Group of composers with nine missing? (3,3)

Definition: Group of composers · charade

  • missing → LESS the arithmetic word for taking away, as in ten less two
  • nine → IX nine written in Roman numerals

The answer splits one way as a name and another as arithmetic: three and three on the page, four and two in the wordplay.

Down

1 down PRADA

Acting students under pressure from fashion house (5)

Definition: fashion house · charade

  • pressure → P P is the physicist's symbol for pressure
  • Acting students → RADA RADA is the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art, the London school where acting students train

The surface is a drama school under strain; the two halves belong to completely different worlds, which is what makes the join hard to see.

2 down OSTRICH

Someone avoiding the truth making surprised comment about precise cut (7)

Definition: Someone avoiding the truth · container + deletion

  • surprised comment → OH 'Oh!' is what you say when something takes you by surprise, and it splits round what it holds
  • precise → STRIC STRICT means precise, minus its last letter

'About' tempts you into turning something round; here it does the quieter job of meaning 'around'.

3 down EXPEL

Better to have change of heart and drive off (5)

Definition: drive off · substitution

  • Better → EXCEL to better a rival is to excel — the verb, not the comparative adjective the surface offers
  • change of heart → P the central letter of that five-letter verb makes way and P moves in

'Better' reads as an adjective in the surface — a better driver — while the wordplay wants the verb, to outdo someone.

5 down AFTERMATH

Period in a substantial hospital – and what follows? (9)

Definition: what follows · container + charade

  • a → A the article, taken as itself
  • substantial → FAT a substantial cheque is a fat one
  • Period → TERM a term is a period of time — school terms, a term of office
  • hospital → H H is the road-sign abbreviation for hospital

A 'math' is an old word for a mowing of grass: the aftermath was the second crop of the year, and only later the consequences of anything.

6 down AMORINI

Shade of gold seen in a small group of cupids? (7)

Definition: group of cupids · container

  • a → A the article, taken as itself
  • small → MINI a mini is the small version of anything — a mini break, a mini bar
  • Shade of gold → OR OR is gold in heraldry, the tincture painted yellow on a coat of arms

'Shade of gold' looks like it wants a colour word; heraldry's OR is the gold the setter means.

7 down SEMANTEME

Source of idea almost appeared to enthral male soldier (9)

Definition: Source of idea · deletion + container

  • appeared → SEEME SEEMED is appeared; almost all of it survives, and the rest then splits open
  • male → M M is the standard abbreviation for male
  • soldier → ANT soldier ants are the fighting caste of a colony, so a soldier can be one

The insect is the crossword's favourite soldier, and the definition is a linguist's term — an unfriendly pair to meet in the same clue.

9 down CONSECUTIVE

Tory having a lot of sex with a lot of babes including five in a row (11)

Definition: in a row · charade + deletion + container

  • Tory → CON a Tory is a Conservative, printed CON on British election results
  • sex → SE most of the word, with the final letter left off
  • babes → CUTIE babes are cuties, and again the last letter goes
  • five → V V is five in Roman numerals

Phi's surface is deliberately racy and every risqué word is doing sober work: a political party, two curtailments and a Roman numeral.

13 down EFFLUVIUM

Swear, dear? I will have show of hesitation: it pollutes the air (9)

Definition: it pollutes the air · charade

  • Swear → EFF to eff is to swear, as in effing and blinding
  • dear? → LUV 'luv' is how a British shopkeeper addresses the customer she calls dear
  • I → I the pronoun taken as its own letter
  • show of hesitation → UM 'um' is the noise you make while you hesitate

Four small pieces, three of them noises people actually make: a Latin-looking word assembled from swearing, endearment and dithering.

14 down REFLEXIVE

Official, as it happens, embracing former lover by instinct (9)

Definition: by instinct · charade + container

  • Official → REF the official in charge of a football match is the ref
  • as it happens → LIVE a broadcast going out as it happens is live
  • former lover → EX an ex is the lover you used to have

Set aside the grammar-book sense about verbs turning back on their subject — the definition wants the knee-jerk one.

16 down URETHRA

You are getting over treatment for heart and internal duct (7)

Definition: internal duct · charade + anagram

  • You are → UR UR is the text-message spelling of 'you are'
  • heart → ETHRA the five letters of HEART, shuffled into a new order

The surface reads as a course of cardiac care; the anatomy the answer names is somewhere else entirely.

17 down THERMOS

Going around Channel Island, drunk overturned source of drink (7)

Definition: source of drink · container + reversal

  • drunk → TOS a sot is a habitual drunk, and here he is tipped over
  • Channel Island → HERM Herm is one of the smallest Channel Islands, just off Guernsey

Setters love the Channel Islands for their short names — Sark, Herm and Alderney all turn up as letter chunks.

20 down TILDE

Mark on letter - Scrabble letter - covering two-pointer? (5)

Definition: Mark on letter · container

  • Scrabble letter → TILE in Scrabble every letter arrives on a wooden tile
  • two-pointer → D D and G are the tiles worth two points in the game

The Scrabble scores are the whole of this clue: only two letters are worth two points, and only one of them fits.

21 down INDEX

Finger one subsequently cut handling tip of dagger (5)

Definition: Finger · charade + deletion + container + first letter

  • one → I I is one in Roman numerals
  • subsequently → NEX NEXT means subsequently, with its last letter cut
  • tip of dagger → D the opening letter of dagger

Four instructions in five letters, and 'tip' is the giveaway: a dagger's point is a letter here, not a blade.
