Across

1 across PARADE

Notice boring dock procession (6)

Definition: procession · container

  • dock → PARE to dock a tail is to pare it, cutting it back, and the word splits open to take the other piece
  • Notice → AD a notice in a paper is an ad, the crossword's usual two letters for one

The surface is a slow morning at the harbour, where a dock is a quay. It is the other dock, the verb for cutting something short.

4 across ARTHUR

Second half of Heston film follows technique of legendary Englishman (6)

Definition: legendary Englishman · charade + last letters

  • technique → ART the art of something is the technique of doing it — the art of conversation
  • Heston film → HUR Charlton Heston starred in Ben-Hur, and the back half of that title is what is wanted

The film is the 1959 chariot-race epic, and the man defined at the end is the king this whole puzzle is built around.

9 across RAGU

Meat sauce for a gumbo's stuffing (4)

Definition: Meat sauce · hidden word

  • for a gumbo's → RAGU four consecutive letters straddle the join, starting at the end of one word and running into the next

Two cuisines in one line: gumbo is the Louisiana stew and the sauce wanted is Italian, so the food talk is misdirection and only the letters matter.

10 across VOICEMAILS

Articulate men delivered sound messages (10)

Definition: sound messages · charade + homophone

  • Articulate → VOICE to articulate a complaint is to voice it
  • men → MAILS men are males, and that word said aloud is spelt this other way

'Sound' looks like the signal for something heard and belongs to the definition instead; the word doing that job is 'delivered', which is what happens to a speech.

11 across MAIDEN

Virgin mother back from Bali hideaway (6)

Definition: Virgin · charade + last letter

  • mother → MA ma is the informal word for mother
  • Bali → I the last letter of the island's name
  • hideaway → DEN a den is the hideaway an animal or a schemer retreats to

The travel-brochure surface hides an ordinary charade, and 'back' is picking a letter rather than describing anybody's journey home.

12 across ELECTRIC

Charged in to vote for hoax, essentially (8)

Definition: Charged · charade + middle letters

  • vote for → ELECT to vote for a candidate is to elect them
  • hoax → RIC a hoax is a trick, and the three letters at the centre of that word are what remain

The surface is a rush to the polling station. 'Charged' is not the mood of the crowd but current in a wire, and it is the definition.

13 across EXCALIBUR

Sword, axe, club and fire essentially deployed (9)

Definition: Sword · anagram + middle letters

  • axe, club → AXECLUB two more weapons hand over seven of the nine letters
  • fire → IR the middle pair of that four-letter word

A list of weapons is exactly where a sword belongs, which is why the fodder hides so well: the first word is the definition and the rest is raw material.

15 across KINGFISHER

Bird who keeps 17 advancing chess piece (10)

Definition: Bird · charade + cycling

  • chess piece → KING the piece the whole game is played to protect, sent forward to the head of the assembly
  • who keeps 17 → FISHER in Arthurian legend the wounded guardian of 17 is the Fisher King, and this is the half of his title left behind

The keeper of the Grail is the Fisher King, and the clue swaps his two halves round. 'Bird' sits alone at the front and is the whole definition.

16 across COCK

Chief swaggerer shown by sources of Chretien on chivalry? Kay (4)

Definition: Chief swaggerer · first letters

  • Chretien on chivalry? Kay → COCK four words in a row, each handing over the letter it begins with

Chretien de Troyes wrote the first Arthurian romances and Kay is Arthur's foster-brother, so the question in the surface is really a list of four initials.

17 across HOLY GRAIL

Lady exhausted after beginning to hurdle over grand track for fabulous cup (4,5)

Definition: fabulous cup · charade + first letter + outer letters

  • hurdle → H the first letter of the obstacle a runner clears
  • over → O O is the cricket scorecard's short form for an over, the set of six balls a bowler sends down
  • Lady → LY the first and last letters of the word, all that is left when the middle goes
  • grand → G G is the standard short form for grand
  • track → RAIL a railway track is a rail

The surface is an athletics meeting, right down to the hurdle and the track, and every one of those sporting words is really an abbreviation or a fragment.

21 across EMPORIUM

Penguin could be leaving queen on island with hesitation for a big shop (8)

Definition: a big shop · deletion + charade

  • Penguin could be → EMPEROR the emperor is the biggest species of penguin, and 'could be' flags one example of the bird
  • queen → ER ER is Elizabeth Regina, the crossword's usual queen, and these are the two letters that go
  • island → I I is the standard short form for island on a map
  • hesitation → UM um is the noise you make while you hesitate

Penguins and queens sit together so comfortably in the Antarctic surface that the instruction hides in plain sight; the rest is ordinary charade joinery saying what it means.

22 across NITWIT

Discovered "in it" with donkey (6)

Definition: donkey · hidden word

  • "in it" with → NITWIT six consecutive letters run from inside the quotation into the word after it, quotation marks and all

The quotation marks look as if they are flagging a phrase to work on. They are only there to hold the surface up, and the letters run straight through them.

24 across SLIM CHANCE

Cancels him after playing around? Not very likely (4,6)

Definition: Not very likely · anagram

  • Cancels him → CANCELSHIM ten letters handed over whole, waiting to be rearranged

The question mark is the setter admitting that his story of someone dropped after messing about is doing double duty as an instruction.

25 across BRIE

British idea evens out French wheel? (4)

Definition: French wheel · charade + alternate letters

  • British → BR BR is the standard short form for British
  • idea → IE the first and third letters of the word, once the second and fourth have gone

'Evens out' reads as smoothing something over and is really an instruction to throw letters away. The question mark covers a definition by shape, not by taste.

26 across TUNDRA

Tough cockney fanatic turned around desolate region (6)

Definition: desolate region · charade + deletion + reversal

  • fanatic → TUN a fanatic is a nut, and the word is read here from its end
  • Tough → DRA tough is hard, minus the aitch, and that too is read from its end

A hard nut is the tough customer the surface is describing, and the pair only work once the aitch has gone and the whole thing is read the other way.

27 across AS WELL

A fop to boot (2,4)

Definition: to boot · charade

  • A → A the article is taken at face value and starts things off
  • fop → SWELL Victorian slang for a dandy, the flashily dressed gentleman about town — the same word as a rising sea

Four short words, no indicator and no joke to unpick: the whole difficulty is seeing that the split falls after the very first letter.

Down

1 down PHALANX

Two princes and an unknown in formation (7)

Definition: formation · charade

  • Two princes → PHAL P is the standard short form for prince, and Hal is the prince of Shakespeare's Henry IV — one behind the other
  • and an → AN the article is taken at face value and joins the queue
  • unknown → X algebra's unknown quantity is x, the letter you solve for

Prince Hal is Shakespeare's Henry V before the crown, and the other prince is only his initial. The formation wanted is the Greek block of spearmen with shields locked.

2 down ROUND TABLE

Song for group with tenor fit for conference (5,5)

Definition: conference · charade

  • Song for group → ROUND a round is the song several voices sing in canon, each starting a line after the last — Frere Jacques is one
  • tenor → T T is the standard short form for tenor on a score
  • fit → ABLE someone fit for a job is able to do it

Two musical words in a row make the surface a choir practice, and only the first of them is a song. The furniture the phrase names belongs to this puzzle's king.

3 down DA VINCI

Six involved in endless caper by international artist (2,5)

Definition: artist · container + deletion + charade

  • endless caper → DANC to caper is to dance, and the last letter has been lopped off
  • Six → VI the Roman numeral for six
  • international → I I is the standard short form for international, as on a footballer's record

'Caper' looks like an anagram signal and is a word for a dance instead. Leonardo is filed under where he came from, the Tuscan village of Vinci.

5 down REEFER

Free, free, free at heart, uplifted with joint (6)

Definition: joint · anagram + middle letters + reversal

  • free → REEF the same four letters as the word before it, rebuilt into the ridge of coral a ship runs onto
  • free at heart → ER the middle pair of that word is RE, and up the page it reads the other way round

Three frees in a row and each does a different job: one gives the instruction, one gives the letters, one gives up its middle.

6 down HEALTHIER

The lady guarding a lake (the round one) is out of the woods? (9)

Definition: out of the woods · container + charade

  • The lady → HER 'the lady' is her, and those three letters split apart to wrap the rest
  • a lake → AL the article gives A, and L is the map-maker's short form for lake
  • the round one → THIE one is I in Roman figures, with 'the' wrapped around it

The bracket holds a container inside a container, which is the whole difficulty. The question mark admits that being past the danger is not quite the same as being well.

7 down ROLLING

Bread with gin drunk by revolutionary (7)

Definition: revolutionary · charade + anagram

  • Bread → ROLL a roll is the small round loaf that comes with soup
  • gin → ING the same three letters, stood in a new order

'Bread with gin drunk' paints a whole evening, but only one of those words is a synonym and the other is raw material. The definition is the single word at the end.

8 down TIME OUT OF MIND

Punishment for toddler concerning watch from ages ago (4,3,2,4)

Definition: from ages ago · charade

  • Punishment for toddler → TIMEOUT a time out is the naughty-step penalty handed to a small child
  • concerning → OF 'of' does the same job: a book of birds is a book concerning birds
  • watch → MIND asked to mind the children, you watch them

The phrase is old legal English for a right so ancient that no living memory reaches back to its start — nothing to do with the naughty step of the surface.

14 down ANCHORMAN

Presenter of careless anachronism is missing (9)

Definition: Presenter · anagram + deletion

  • anachronism → ANACHRONM the long word with the two letters of 'is' already taken out, ready to be reordered

An anachronism is a thing out of its own time, so the surface grumbles about a wristwatch in a period drama; the word itself is the fodder.

16 down CAMELOT

Appeared before crowd in musical (7)

Definition: musical · charade

  • Appeared → CAME someone who appeared at the party came to it
  • crowd → LOT a lot of people is a crowd of them, as in 'that lot outside'

The show's title later became the nickname for the Kennedy White House, and the castle behind it is the seat this puzzle keeps circling.

18 down YANKEES

Petition for vote against stuck up Americans (7)

Definition: Americans · charade + reversal

  • vote against → YAN a vote against is a nay, and in a down entry it climbs the grid
  • Petition for → KEES to petition for a thing is to seek it, and those letters climb too

Both words are written out in clue order and the whole assembly is then read upwards, which is why the two halves seem to have swapped places.

19 down INITIAL

First name shortened (7)

Definition: First · double definition

  • First → the adjective: earliest, the opening one before any others
  • name shortened → the noun: what is left of a name when only its starting letter is written

Three words that read as one phrase, a shortened first name, and the split falls after the very first word, with each half defining the same seven letters.
