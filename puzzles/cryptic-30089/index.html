Guardian Cryptic Crossword No 30,089

Across

8 across YOGI BEAR

Flexible practitioner to support animated character (4,4)

Definition: animated character · charade

  • Flexible practitioner → YOGI the flexible practitioner is a yogi, the one who does the yoga
  • to support → BEAR to bear a weight is to support it

The surface promises a bendy expert holding something up; the answer is the picnic-basket thief of Jellystone Park.

9 across ASSET

Strength from dog shaking off lead (5)

Definition: Strength · deletion

  • dog → BASSET the basset hound is the dog
  • lead → B a dog's lead here is its leading letter, the B at the front, which goes

Every word of the surface is about walking a dog, and 'lead' is the one that changes job: leash on top, first letter underneath.

10 across ANON

Year in Spanish Main’s ending soon (4)

Definition: soon · charade + last letter

  • Year in Spanish → ANO año is Spanish for year
  • Main’s → N the last letter of Main

The Spanish Main was the Caribbean coast of the empire, which is why the two halves of the wordplay read as one place name.

11 across EL SALVADOR

Disney princess left queen over trouble in country (2,8)

Definition: country · charade + container

  • Disney princess → ELSA Elsa, the ice queen of Frozen
  • left → L L is the standard abbreviation for left
  • queen → VR Victoria Regina, VR, the crossword's queen
  • trouble → ADO much ado about nothing — ado is trouble, and it goes inside VR

VR for Victoria Regina is worth banking — setters reach for royal cyphers constantly — and a Disney princess keeps the surface in the snow rather than in Central America.

12 across DANIEL

Book that is set in strange land (6)

Definition: Book · anagram + container

  • land → DANL the four letters of LAND, rearranged
  • that is → IE i.e. is the Latin shortening of 'that is'

'Book' in a cryptic is very often one of the Bible's, and this is the prophet who spent a night in with the lions.

14 across MILLWALL

Football team crush defensive formation (8)

Definition: Football team · charade

  • crush → MILL to mill grain is to crush it
  • defensive formation → WALL the wall of players lined up in front of a free kick

Both halves of the wordplay are football words too, which is what makes the join invisible — and the club's nickname is the Lions.

16 across INKPADS

North Korea’s leader hacking into computer’s digital printing devices (7)

Definition: digital printing devices · container

  • computer’s → IPADS the computer is an iPad, and the setter's ’s hands over the S
  • North → N N is the compass abbreviation for north
  • Korea’s leader → K the leader of KOREA is its first letter

'Digital' is the pivot: not electronic here but of the fingers, which is why a state hacking story ends up at a fingerprint.

18 across NOBLEST

Most generous and good of Poirot to make a comeback in case (7)

Definition: Most generous · charade + reversal

  • good of Poirot → NOB Poirot is Belgian, so his word for good is BON, arriving backwards
  • in case → LEST lest means in case, as in 'lest we forget'

'In case' looks like joinery holding the sentence together and is really four of the seven letters, which is where the clue hides.

21 across CLARENCE

Carriage clock’s face cutting clumsy cleaner (8)

Definition: Carriage · anagram + first letter

  • clock’s face → C the face of the clock is the front of the word, its C
  • cleaner → CLEANER anagram fodder, seven letters

The surface is a domestic scene with a clock and a hopeless cleaner; the answer is Victorian transport named after a royal duke.

23 across ALEXIA

Personal assistant admits one form of word blindness (6)

Definition: form of word blindness · container

  • Personal assistant → ALEXA Amazon's Alexa is the personal assistant on the shelf
  • one → I one is the Roman numeral I

A voice assistant that reads things out for you is a neat surface for a condition about not being able to read.

24 across RED ADMIRAL

Distressed maid in pantry retreating from flying insect (3,7)

Definition: flying insect · anagram + container + reversal

  • pantry → LARDER the pantry is the larder, which retreats to REDRAL
  • maid → ADMI the letters of MAID, distressed, and dropped inside REDRAL

A distressed maid in a pantry is a whole Victorian novel in five words, and not one of them is about a butterfly.

26 across CAGE

Heartless bum in prison (4)

Definition: prison · deletion

  • bum → CADGE to bum a cigarette is to cadge one; the heart of it, D, goes

'Bum' is the verb here, not the noun the surface is pointing at: to bum a fiver is to cadge one.

27 across INCAN

Hotel California charges old person (5)

Definition: old person · container

  • Hotel → INN the hotel is an inn
  • California → CA CA is the postal abbreviation for California

The Eagles' song holds the surface up, and the answer is an inn with a state loaded into it. CA for California is worth banking.

28 across IN NO TIME

I mention getting hammered before you can say Jack Robinson (2,2,4)

Definition: before you can say Jack Robinson · anagram

  • I mention → IMENTION anagram fodder, eight letters

The definition is the whole idiom at the end, which is why it reads as surface; 'I mention' does nothing for the sentence and everything for the letters.

Down

1 down MOUNTAIN

Benny Hill? (8)

Definition: Benny Hill? · cryptic definition

  • Benny → The surface hands you the comedian; the setter is thinking of BEN, which is what Scotland puts in front of its highest lumps — Nevis, Lomond.
  • Hill? → So the question the clue is really asking is what you call a hill grand enough to be a Ben, with the question mark owning the upgrade.

There is nothing to take apart here — no fodder, no letters to move. Benny Hill was the comedian; Ben Nevis is the parse.

2 down LION

Hero knocked over nuclear fuel (4)

Definition: Hero · charade + reversal

  • nuclear → N N is the standard shortening of nuclear, as in N-bomb
  • fuel → OIL oil is the fuel — and N + OIL, knocked over, is the answer

'Nuclear fuel' reads as one thing on the surface and is two abbreviations stacked underneath, which is the whole trick of a four-letter clue.

3 down RESEAL

11 stopping Spanish team getting close again (6)

Definition: close again · container

  • Spanish team → REAL Real Madrid, Real Betis, Real Sociedad — Spain's leagues are full of Reals
  • 11 → ES 11 across is EL SALVADOR, and the country goes about on ES plates

A cross-reference number stands for another entry in this grid, so 11 has to be solved before this one gives up its two letters.

4 down PRESUME

Suppose Big Bird’s turning up to support Trump? (7)

Definition: Suppose · charade + reversal

  • Trump? → PRES Trump is a president, PRES for short — the question mark admits it is one example
  • Big Bird’s → UME the big bird is an EMU, climbing the column

Big Bird and Trump are both real people-shaped things on the surface, and neither is wanted: the clue needs a flightless bird and an abbreviation.

5 down MAUL

Mother starts to upset Lily Savage (4)

Definition: Savage · charade + first letters

  • Mother → MA MA is the short form of mother
  • upset Lily → UL the opening letters of Upset Lily

Lily Savage was Paul O'Grady's drag act, which is what the capital letter is for; the clue wants 'savage' as a plain verb.

6 down OSCAR WILDE

Award sounded crazy for playwright (5,5)

Definition: playwright · charade + homophone

  • Award → OSCAR the Academy Award is an Oscar
  • crazy → WILDE crazy is WILD, and WILD said aloud is how the surname is heard

Wilde wrote The Importance of Being Earnest, which 13 down is quietly sitting next to. The silent E is the entire homophone.

7 down STROLL

Bad-tempered types last to get up for walk (6)

Definition: walk · cycling

  • Bad-tempered types → TROLLS trolls are the bad-tempered types, under bridges and under the line

This is a rotation, not a reversal: only one letter travels and the rest hold their order. Turned round properly it would read SLLORT.

13 down IMPORTANCE

Value of New Romantic EP (10)

Definition: Value · anagram

  • Romantic EP → ROMANTICEP anagram fodder, ten letters

New Romantic bands and their EPs make a tidy 1981 surface, and the capital R is pure disguise — it is just the fodder's first letter.

15 down LEO

Shot regularly dismissed Ollie Pope (3)

Definition: Pope · anagram + alternate letters

  • Ollie → OLE drop the second and fourth letters of OLLIE and OLE is what is left

Ollie Pope bats for England, so the surface is a morning at the Oval. The answer is a papal name, and a lion besides.

17 down DEN

Study of depression cut short (3)

Definition: Study · deletion

  • depression → DENT a dent is a depression in a surface, and it loses its last letter

'Study' is the room, not the act of learning — and a den is also where the animal running through this grid lives.

19 down SKIN GAME

Swindling trick from man in uniform (4,4)

Definition: Swindling trick · container

  • uniform → SAME uniform means all alike, the same throughout
  • man → KING the king is a man on the chessboard

'Uniform' is the adjective, not the soldier's outfit the surface promises, and that swap is the whole clue.

20 down DETROIT

Denis Villeneuve’s right to film movie in US city (7)

Definition: US city · container

  • Denis Villeneuve’s right → DROIT Villeneuve is a Québécois director, and droit is French for right
  • movie → ET Spielberg's E.T., the shortest movie in the crossword's stock

A director shooting a film is the surface, and both halves of the wordplay keep it: his French 'right' and a two-letter picture.

22 down LIEBIG

Chemist replacing parts of large Whopper (6)

Definition: Chemist · charade + cycling

  • large → BIG large is big
  • Whopper → LIE a whopper is an outrageous lie; the burger is there for the surface

Justus von Liebig is worth banking: his name is on the condenser in every school laboratory and on the meat extract that became Oxo.

23 down ASLANT

2 followed by Tesla obliquely (6)

Definition: obliquely · charade

  • 2 → ASLAN the answer to 2 down is a lion, and Narnia's is Aslan
  • Tesla → T T is the symbol for the tesla, the unit of magnetic flux density

Tesla the car firm is the misdirection; the unit named after Nikola Tesla is what the wordplay wants, and it is a single letter.

25 down MANE

Artist trimmed long hair (4)

Definition: long hair · deletion

  • Artist → MANET Manet, the French painter of Olympia, with the end trimmed off

Manet and Monet are both four-fifths of something else, which is why the crossword reaches for them so often.

26 down CATS

Musical Youth finally escaping from Jaws (4)

Definition: Musical · deletion + last letter

  • Jaws → CHATS to jaw is to talk away, so 'jaws' is CHATS
  • Youth → H the closing letter of YOUTH, which escapes

Musical Youth had the hit and Jaws had the shark, so the surface is all pop culture and neither half means what it seems to.
