Independent Cryptic Crossword No 12,430

Across

7 across YOUNGER

More recent winner of Pop Idol seen by European river (7)

Definition: More recent · charade

  • winner of Pop Idol → YOUNG Will Young won the first series of Pop Idol
  • European → E E is the standard short form of European
  • river → R R is river on any map

This one asks you to remember a talent show result from 2002, and then only wants the surname of the singer who won it.

8 across CHAPPIE

Man taps phone program that is ... (7)

Definition: Man · charade

  • taps → CH the pair over a sink are labelled cold and hot
  • phone program → APP an app is the program you run on a phone
  • that is → IE i.e. is the Latin id est, that is

Taps are a setter's way of asking for the two letters on the sink, and the dots at the end only pass the sentence on to 10 across.

10 across PATIENCE

... surprisingly neat epic game (8)

Definition: game · anagram

  • neat epic → NEATEPIC the eight letters of both words are the raw material

The dots leading in from 8 across are surface only — each clue stands on its own — and the answer is the card game a solitary player deals out.

11 across LARIAT

Burglar I attacked holding rope (6)

Definition: rope · hidden word

  • Burglar I attacked → LARIAT the run starts in burgLAR and finishes in I ATtacked

The words read as a snatch of courtroom testimony, and that capital I is exactly what stops the eye seeing a run of letters.

12 across INEXPEDIENT

Unsuitable pix? I need ten developed (11)

Definition: Unsuitable · anagram

  • pix → PIX pix is the trade's slang for photographs
  • I need ten → INEEDTEN the three small words go into the mix exactly as they stand

The whole surface is a darkroom, so the instruction looks like part of the picture rather than the order it is giving.

15 across PEAR

Conference maybe cut back we hear (4)

Definition: Conference maybe · homophone

  • cut back → PEAR to pare back is to cut, and PARE said aloud is spelled the other way

'Maybe' is the setter flagging an example rather than a synonym, since Conference is one named variety among many.

17 across CLING

Hold fast having caught fish (5)

Definition: Hold fast · charade

  • caught → C c is caught on a cricket scorecard
  • fish → LING the ling is a long fish of the cod family

C for caught comes off a cricket scorecard, and the ling is the cod-family fish that grids keep permanently in stock.

18 across CIAO

See you appearing regularly in Chicago (4)

Definition: See you · alternate letters

  • Chicago → CIAO ChIcAgO, taking the first letter and every other one after it

The word serves as both hello and goodbye in Italian; 'See you' picks the parting one, and the city is only there to carry the letters.

19 across PLAYS TRUANT

A trusty plan devised for what Ferris Bueller does (5,6)

Definition: what Ferris Bueller does · anagram

  • A trusty plan → ATRUSTYPLAN all eleven letters of the phrase are the raw material

Ferris Bueller's Day Off is a whole film about a boy skiving school, so the definition is a clause pointing at one action.

22 across OCTROI

Tax return on investment after 31 days (6)

Definition: Tax · charade

  • 31 days → OCT October is a 31-day month, shortened to Oct
  • return on investment → ROI ROI is the financier's return on investment

A month clued by its length is a standing device: seven of them have 31 days, so the crossing letters are what pick October out.

23 across ON THE JOB

At work dealing with article and book (2,3,3)

Definition: At work · charade

  • dealing with → ON a book on birds is a book dealing with birds
  • article → THE the definite article
  • book → JOB Job is a book of the Old Testament

Two staples in one clue: an article is the grammarian's THE, and a book is very often one of the Bible's.

25 across PATELLA

Meat spread all around bone (7)

Definition: bone · charade + reversal

  • Meat spread → PATE pate is the meat spread you put on toast
  • all → ALL the little word taken whole, before it is turned

Meat spread all around sounds like a buffet table, which is what keeps the next word looking like description rather than instruction.

26 across QUECHUA

Which Spanish friend finally abandoned a Peruvian? (7)

Definition: Peruvian · charade + deletion

  • Which Spanish → QUE que is the Spanish for which
  • friend finally abandoned → CHU CHUM is a friend, and its closing M is abandoned
  • a → A the little word joins on at the end as it stands

Setters lean on the Spanish everybody half-knows, and the question mark admits that a Quechua speaker is only one sort of Peruvian.

Down

1 down WOMANISER

Lothario's shrewder when touring gulf state (9)

Definition: Lothario · container

  • shrewder → WISER shrewder and wiser are the same comparative
  • gulf state → OMAN Oman is the sultanate on the Gulf, the state crosswords reach for most

Comparatives are useful to a setter: 'shrewder' has to become a whole word rather than an abbreviation, which is a lot of letters handed over at once.

2 down ENTICE

Lead astray some apprentices (6)

Definition: Lead astray · hidden word

  • apprentices → ENTICE the run sits in the middle of apprENTICEs

Letters buried inside one long word are harder to spot than letters straddling two, because there is no join for the eye to catch on.

3 down LEAN

Unprofitable bank (4)

Definition: Unprofitable · double definition

  • Unprofitable → LEAN a stretch of time with nothing spare in it, as in the years right after a bad harvest
  • bank → LEAN an aircraft banks by tipping over to one side

Two words, no indicator and nothing to take apart: that shape is nearly always two definitions, and here the second is a pilot's verb, not a high street noun.

4 down CHALLENGER

Test top of rainwater tank (10)

Definition: tank · charade + first letter

  • Test → CHALLENGE a test of your ability is a challenge
  • top of rainwater → R the first letter of rainwater

The tank in the surface is a water butt; the tank in the definition is the Army's, and that switch is the whole clue.

5 down OPERATIC

Histrionic pub server describes age (8)

Definition: Histrionic · container

  • pub server → OPTIC the optic is the measure clamped under a spirits bottle behind the bar
  • age → ERA an era is an age

'Pub server' sounds like a person and is really a piece of bar equipment, which is where this one hides.

6 down MILAN

Motorway network in Italian city (5)

Definition: Italian city · charade

  • Motorway → MI the M1 spelled out as two characters, motorway one
  • network → LAN a LAN is the local area network wired into any office

Two conventions worth banking here: a motorway becomes MI, letter beside letter, and a network in a grid is almost always the office LAN.

9 down READMISSION

Study purpose for return to hospital? (11)

Definition: return to hospital · charade

  • Study → READ to read a subject at university is to study it
  • purpose → MISSION a mission is the purpose someone is sent out with

The question mark is the setter admitting that his definition describes the answer rather than swapping for it word for word.

13 down PICCALILLI

Film title about independent group of soldiers in a pickle (10)

Definition: a pickle · charade + container

  • Film → PIC a pic is a film, the trade's own shortening
  • title → CALL to call a thing is to give it its title
  • independent → I I is independent on a ballot paper
  • group of soldiers → LI LI is the Light Infantry, as in the DLI

Being in a pickle means being in trouble, which is the trap: the pickle wanted here is the jar of yellow relish.

14 down GASTROPUB

Pastor with bug is tipsy in posh boozer (9)

Definition: posh boozer · anagram

  • Pastor → PASTOR the six letters of the clergyman go into the mix
  • bug → BUG the insect's three letters join them

A tipsy pastor is a good enough picture to stop you counting, and the count is the tell: the two words hold exactly nine letters.

16 down REPORTER

Writer raised hesitation over old railway worker (8)

Definition: Writer · charade + reversal

  • hesitation → ER er is the noise you make while you hesitate
  • old railway worker → PORTER the porter who carried luggage on the platform, a job the railways no longer keep

'Old' is not the usual O here: it is telling you the railway job belongs to another era, when somebody carried your case to the train.

20 down AGENCY

Time chairman originally enters City office (6)

Definition: office · charade + container + first letter

  • Time → AGE an age is a long stretch of time
  • City → NY NY is New York City
  • chairman originally → C the first letter of chairman, which is what goes into NY

The city wanted is New York; the boardroom surface leans on that capital C to keep you looking at the Square Mile instead.

21 down OCTAL

Officer commanding old volunteers left base (5)

Definition: base · charade

  • Officer commanding → OC OC is the army's officer commanding
  • old volunteers → TA the Territorial Army, the volunteer force renamed the Army Reserve in 2014
  • left → L L is left

'Base' is not a barracks: octal is the base-eight counting system computing grew up on, and the parade ground is there to hide that sense.

24 down THUG

Tough husband aboard boat (4)

Definition: Tough · container

  • boat → TUG a tug is the little boat that shoves ships about
  • husband → H H is husband in the abbreviation book

'Tough' is a noun in the definition while the surface reads it as an adjective, and that shift is the whole of the disguise.
