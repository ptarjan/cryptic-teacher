Independent Sunday Cryptic Crossword No 1,901

Across

1 across COMPRESS

Squeeze Mike Pence in hearts and spades (8)

Definition: Squeeze · container + charade

  • hearts → CORES the heart of a matter is its core, and these letters split open after the first two
  • Mike → M Mike is how the radio alphabet says M
  • Pence → P p is pence on any British price tag
  • spades → S S is the bridge scorecard's letter for spades

The vice-president is a decoy assembled out of a radio-alphabet letter and a coin, and only one of the two card suits in the surface is really being used as a suit.

6 across SCALP

What might be left inside hat after opening of sutures? (5)

Definition: What might be left inside hat after opening of sutures? · &lit + charade + container + first letter

  • sutures → S only the first letter of sutures is wanted
  • hat → CAP a cap is a hat, and it opens up between its second and third letters
  • left → L L is the standard abbreviation for left

Read once it is an operating theatre; read again it is three pieces of shorthand. Both readings use the same words, which is what makes it an all-in-one.

9 across PIT STOP

Remove stones from pockets, reversing element of race strategy (3,4)

Definition: element of race strategy · charade + reversal

  • Remove stones from → PIT to pit a cherry is to take the stone out of it
  • pockets → POTS a snooker player who pockets a ball pots it

Both halves of the wordplay are verbs pretending to be a sentence about theft, and the enumeration 3,4 is the first hint that the answer belongs on a racetrack.

10 across OPEN-AIR

Chat GPT's creator runs outside (4-3)

Definition: outside · charade

  • Chat GPT's creator → OPENAI OpenAI is the company that built ChatGPT
  • runs → R R is how a cricket scorer writes runs

Splitting 'Chat GPT' into two words is the misdirection: its creator turns out to be a name lifted whole into the grid rather than a person to identify.

11 across GRILL

Pump beginning to generate trickle of water (5)

Definition: Pump · charade + first letter

  • generate → G just the opening letter of generate
  • trickle of water → RILL a rill is a small stream, the word poets use for a trickle

The surface is plumbing from end to end and both halves of the wordplay play along; the definition is the interrogation-room sense of the verb.

13 across NOTORIOUS

Different from gold, debt instruments reportedly untrustworthy (9)

Definition: reportedly untrustworthy · charade

  • Different from → NOT different from a thing is simply not that thing
  • gold → OR or is the heraldic tincture gold, and a crossword staple
  • debt instruments → IOUS an IOU is a written promise to pay, so several are IOUs

The surface is a market report and every piece plays along with it. OR for gold comes from heraldry and is worth banking — it turns up constantly.

14 across INHOSPITABLE

Plain bothies in disrepair providing no shelter (12)

Definition: providing no shelter · anagram

  • Plain bothies → INHOSPITABLE the twelve letters of PLAIN BOTHIES, rebuilt

A bothy is a rough hut kept unlocked for walkers in the Scottish hills, which is exactly what makes the surface hang together and the definition sting.

18 across COME TO NO GOOD

Orbiter going round Mars, perhaps carrying oxygen tank (4,2,2,4)

Definition: tank · charade + container

  • Orbiter → COMET a comet orbits the sun on a long loop
  • going → ON anything that is going is on — an engine, a light, a show
  • round → O a round is a circle, and O is the crossword's circle
  • Mars, perhaps → GOD Mars is one of the Roman gods, and 'perhaps' says he is an example of one
  • oxygen → O O is the chemical symbol for oxygen, and it goes inside

The enumeration is the misdirection: 4,2,2,4 cuts the letters in three places the wordplay never uses. And Mars here is a Roman god, not the planet an orbiter would visit.

21 across SHOW FIGHT

In a sense, the way force offer resistance (4,5)

Definition: offer resistance · container + charade

  • a sense → SIGHT sight is one of the five senses, and it opens up after its first letter
  • the way → HOW the way you did it is how you did it
  • force → F F is the physicist's symbol for force

'In a sense' reads as a hedge and is really an instruction plus a noun — one of the five senses — and the comma the setter drops after it is what sells the disguise.

23 across ELFIN

Slightly charming son leaves ego at home (5)

Definition: Slightly charming · deletion + charade

  • son → S S is the abbreviation for son, and it is what departs
  • ego → ELF your ego is your self, and with that S gone this is what remains
  • at home → IN someone at home is in

'Slightly' is not a hedge here but a build: slight means small and slender, which is half of what the definition is claiming.

24 across ATTACHE

Diplomat not drinking gripped by advanced thirst (7)

Definition: Diplomat · container + charade

  • advanced → A A is the abbreviation for advanced, as in an A-level
  • not drinking → TT TT is teetotal, the usual shorthand for someone off the drink
  • thirst → ACHE an ache for something is a longing, which is what a thirst for it is

Every piece is a standing abbreviation dressed as description, and 'advanced thirst' is written as one phrase precisely so that you will not split it.

25 across SURPASS

Better teacher said "Ask me another" (7)

Definition: Better · charade + homophone

  • teacher → SUR a schoolmaster is addressed as sir, and out loud that becomes the opening syllable
  • "Ask me another" → PASS pass is what you say on a quiz show when you cannot answer

'Better' at the front is a verb rather than a comparison, and the quoted phrase is the sound of a contestant giving up on a question.

27 across DOWNS

Upland swallows (5)

Definition: Upland · double definition

  • Upland → DOWNS the chalk hills of southern England, from an old word meaning hill
  • swallows → DOWNS to down a pint is to swallow it in one

Two words that look like opposites sit side by side, up in the clue and down in the answer, and that contradiction is the whole of the joke.

28 across MEDITATE

Consider change to script that bores friend (8)

Definition: Consider · container

  • friend → MATE a mate is a friend, and it splits after its first letter
  • change to script → EDIT to edit a script is to change it

'Bores' is doing work rather than commentary: it drills a hole through the middle of a word instead of sending anyone to sleep.

Down

2 down OUTRIGHT

Total calculated wrongly then correctly (8)

Definition: Total · charade

  • calculated wrongly → OUT a sum that has gone astray is out — your figures are out by a mile
  • correctly → RIGHT to get it correctly is to get it right

Both halves are verdicts on a sum, which is what keeps the surface in the accounts office; the definition is doing arithmetic duty too, and is the one word that isn't.

3 down PUT

Temperature to the north rising, say (3)

Definition: say · charade + reversal

  • Temperature → T T is the abbreviation on every thermometer and weather chart
  • to the north → UP north is the top of the map, so it is up

A three-letter down entry offers very little to grip, so the give-away is 'say' sitting after a comma, where an example indicator would usually live.

4 down EXPANSIVE

Talkative dear has answer for everything, initially (9)

Definition: Talkative · substitution

  • dear → EXPENSIVE dear means costly, not beloved — and this is the word that gets altered
  • answer → A A is the standard abbreviation for answer, and it is the letter moving in
  • everything → E the opening letter of everything is the one being turned out

A whole nine-letter word arrives almost intact and one vowel changes hands, which is why the clue feels too short for its answer.

5 down SHORT

Crumbly sandwich without filling worth getting unwrapped? (5)

Definition: Crumbly · charade + outer letters + middle letters

  • sandwich → SH the first and last letters are the bread of the word
  • worth → ORT strip the W and the H off the ends and this is the middle that is left

Every instruction here is disguised as something done to food, which is the whole reason none of them reads as an instruction.

6 down SIERRA LEONE

Land transaction only includes one mistake (6,5)

Definition: Land · charade + container

  • transaction → SALE a sale is the transaction, and it splits after its first letter
  • one → I I is the Roman numeral for one
  • mistake → ERR to err is to make a mistake
  • only → ONE 'one' does the work of 'only' — your one hope is your only hope

The two number words are a deliberate tangle: the one that says 'only' becomes a number, and the one that says 'one' becomes a Roman numeral instead.

7 down AMAZON

Keep stunning shot of English river (6)

Definition: river · charade + deletion

  • stunning → AMAZE to stun someone is to amaze them
  • Keep → ON 'keep' plus a verb is that verb plus 'on' — keep going is go on — so it follows rather than leads
  • English → E E is the abbreviation for English, and this is the letter got rid of

'Keep' looks like a castle and 'stunning shot' like a photograph. The step that unlocks it is hearing 'keep stunning' as an instruction to carry on amazing.

8 down PARISH

Vicar's patch has rip that needs fixing (6)

Definition: Vicar's patch · anagram

  • has rip → PARISH the six letters of HAS RIP, reset in a new order

A torn cassock and a vicar make one continuous picture, so nothing announces itself as a mechanism — and the fodder hides in two of the commonest words in English.

9 down PUGLIA

Dog starts to learn Italian, answering "heel" there? (6)

Definition: "heel" there? · charade + first letters

  • Dog → PUG a pug is a small flat-faced dog
  • learn Italian, answering → LIA the three words hand over their opening letters in order

'There' has no country in front of it: the only Italy in the clue is one of the words being taken apart, so the definition leans on the wordplay for its own subject.

12 down LAST OFFICES

The Spanish aristocrat does sweet decorations for funeral (4,7)

Definition: funeral · charade

  • The Spanish → LAS las is Spanish for 'the' in front of a plural
  • aristocrat → TOFF a toff is a member of the upper classes
  • sweet decorations → ICES to ice a cake is to give it its sweet decoration

Spanish has four words for 'the' and only the feminine plural fits; the cake-and-party surface is what keeps you from seeing where the answer is heading.

15 down AMORTISED

Advert claiming merit so inappropriately paid off (9)

Definition: paid off · container + anagram

  • Advert → AD ad is the everyday short form of advert, and it splits to take the rest inside
  • merit so → MORTISE the seven letters of MERIT SO in a new order

A mortise is the slot a tenon fits into — a joiner's word worth keeping — and the surface is busy pretending to be a boastful advertisement.

16 down BOUFFANT

Boxing match interrupted by supporter after Fury's face puffed up (8)

Definition: puffed up · container + charade + first letter

  • Boxing match → BOUT a bout is a boxing match, and it splits three letters in
  • Fury → F only the front letter of the boxer's name
  • supporter → FAN a fan is a supporter of a team or a fighter

Tyson Fury is a real heavyweight and the surface belongs entirely to him, which is why nothing in it looks like it could be about hairdressing.

17 down ADONIS

Classical dish Chinese-American lawyer served up (6)

Definition: Classical dish · charade + reversal

  • Chinese → SINO Sino- is the combining form for Chinese, as in Sino-Soviet
  • American lawyer → DA a DA is a district attorney, the prosecuting lawyer in an American court

The surface promises a menu and 'served up' plays along with it; the only cookery in the clue is the direction the letters travel.

19 down ISLAND

DIY sale clearing odd bits with kitchen furniture? (6)

Definition: kitchen furniture? · alternate letters + charade

  • DIY sale → ISL dIySaLe hands over its second, fourth and sixth letters
  • with → AND and does the job of with — fish and chips is fish with chips

'DIY sale' reads as a bargain hunt and is really just seven letters to count through; the question mark owns up to the definition naming one kind among many.

20 down KOWTOW

Crawl from Krakow to Warsaw (6)

Definition: Crawl · hidden word

  • Krakow to Warsaw → KOWTOW the run begins three letters from the end of Krakow and stops on the first letter of Warsaw

A journey across Poland is a handsome cover for a word English took from the Chinese court, where the bow touched the forehead to the ground.

22 down GLEAM

Male gymnast's head breaks weak beam (5)

Definition: weak beam · anagram + first letter

  • Male gymnast → GLEAM MALE, plus the G that heads gymnast, rearranged

Nothing marks 'Male' as material rather than description, which is what makes it hide so well; and a gymnast's head here is a letter, not a body part.

26 down ROT

Go off summit in the wrong direction (3)

Definition: Go off · reversal

  • summit → TOR a tor is a rocky hilltop, the Dartmoor word crosswords lean on

'Go off' is the kitchen sense of the phrase, not the departing one — and a summit turned round is the shortest kind of reversal there is.
